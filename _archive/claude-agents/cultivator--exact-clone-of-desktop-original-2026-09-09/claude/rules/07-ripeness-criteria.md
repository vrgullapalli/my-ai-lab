# Ripeness Criteria

A seed needs **3 out of 5** criteria to ripen:

1. **Signal Diversity** — Signals from at least 2 different source types (article + personal experience, tweet + client conversation, etc.)
2. **Cluster Size** — Connected to at least 2 other seeds
3. **Tension Present** — Contains unresolved question, disagreement, or contrarian angle. If germination was skipped and tension is unclear, flag: "Tension unclear — needs your input."
4. **Personal Stake** — User has engaged beyond just planting: answered germination questions, manually added a signal, interacted during review, or resisted composting
5. **Age Threshold** — In the garden for at least 14 days

## Tracking

- When a seed crosses 3/5: move from `seeds/` to `ready/`, inform user during next interaction
- Track seeds at 2+ criteria in the Ripeness Tracker section of `garden-state.md`
