Part gardener, part archivist, part patient observer. GREENHOUSE's job is to make sure your best ideas don't die in your notes app. Plant a seed. See what grows.

---

## What GREENHOUSE Does

Your notes are not a filing cabinet. You capture ideas, organize them, then wonder why they feel dead when you return.

GREENHOUSE is built to do the opposite. Instead of archiving ideas in sealed containers, it tends them like living plants. You drop in raw thoughts, links, observations without deciding what they are or where they go. GREENHOUSE cross-references them, tracks their ripeness over time, and surfaces connections you didn't see.

Ideas move through states: seedling → growing → ripening → ready. Each state needs different care. GREENHOUSE knows when an idea is wilting, when two seeds are actually circling the same question, and when something is finally ready to become content.

Content emerges from ideas you've actually tended, not just captured.

---

## What to Give GREENHOUSE

GREENHOUSE acts as the bridge between raw, half-formed observation and writable content.

Feed it anything that catches your attention:

- A link with or without context
- A shower thought or observation
- A quote that stuck with you
- A conversation fragment
- Something that bothered you about your industry
- A pattern you keep noticing

GREENHOUSE handles the leap from "random input" to "organized, connected idea."

---

## Built For

GREENHOUSE is for anyone with hundreds of notes sitting in isolation, knowing there are connections between them but lacking the system to surface those patterns.

## Core Capabilities

| Capability | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Seed Planting | Accepts ideas directly or syncs from Notion/Obsidian (via MCP) |
| Germination Questions | Captures personal stake and tension at planting time |
| Cross-Referencing | Reads the garden index first, then targeted seed files. Surfaces connections and convergence warnings |
| Ripeness Tracking | 5 criteria (Signal Diversity, Cluster Size, Tension, Personal Stake, Age). 3/5 threshold to move to ready/ |
| State Management | Tracks seedling → growing → ripening → ready, plus wilting (14 days) and orphan (10 days no connections) |
| Composting | Retires ideas gracefully without deletion. Cross-references new seeds against compost for revival |
| User Pattern Learning | Adapts to your planting rhythm, germination engagement, and ripeness response |

## Inputs & Outputs

**Give GREENHOUSE:**

- Any idea, link, or observation (as raw or formed as you like)
- Optional context via germination questions: "What made you notice this?" and "Do you agree or resist it?"

**GREENHOUSE returns:**

- A dashboard showing vital stats, active themes, ripeness tracker, and convergence warnings
- Seeds cross-referenced with existing ideas and composted material
- Clear signal when ideas hit the 3/5 ripeness threshold and move to ready/
- A tending system that remembers you between sessions

---

## What GREENHOUSE Is NOT For

GREENHOUSE is an idea-tending system, not a writing assistant.

The system is designed to track, connect, and ripen ideas until they're ready to become content. It will not write the final draft for you, nor will it research topics unprompted.

The judgment of which ripened idea becomes your next piece is yours. GREENHOUSE gives you a cleaner table to work from. You don't get fewer decisions to make, just better-informed ones.

---

## How to Use GREENHOUSE

**Four commands:**

| Say this | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **GREENHOUSE** | Dashboard. See vital stats, active themes, ripeness tracker, convergence warnings, orphan watch |
| **Plant** | Add a new idea directly or sync from your notes app |
| **Ripen** | Check what's closest to becoming real content, see which criteria are met/missing |
| **Compost** | Review wilting and orphaned seeds, decide what to retire or revive |

**First run:** The onboarding wizard starts automatically. No setup command needed.

**Tips:**
- Don't overthink planting. Drop anything that catches your attention. The garden does the organizing.
- Skip germination questions if you want. The agent won't nag. But answering them makes seeds ripen faster.
- Check "GREENHOUSE" weekly to stay oriented. The agent notices patterns you won't.
- Trust the compost. Composted ideas aren't gone. They'll resurface if a new seed connects to them.

---

## Limitations

- GREENHOUSE only reads from external sources. It doesn't hunt for them. If you need a research agent that gathers from outside, that's Watson.
- The system rewards patience. Ideas need at least 14 days to hit the age threshold. Fast content cycles won't align with the greenhouse rhythm.
- Ripeness is tracked, not guaranteed. The agent tells you what's ready, but you decide what to write.
- Cross-referencing quality depends on how consistently you plant seeds. Sparse gardens yield fewer connections.
