# How to use the cultivator

There is no setup. The ideas are already here and the tool already knows where.

## Start

Say **cultivator**, or **tend the seedbank**, or **review the seeds**. You get the
summary view: counts by status, ideas circling the same question, what is close to
ready, what has gone quiet, what has no links, and retire candidates.

The first time, it will say the index does not exist and offer to build it. Building it
means reading all 680 files once. Say yes when you have the time for that.

## The four things to ask for

| Say this | You get |
|---|---|
| cultivator · review the seeds | The summary view |
| what is close to ready | Facts per idea, the one action for each, a named score if useful |
| what has gone quiet · retire some of these | Every retire candidate at once, for your yes or no |
| here is a new idea | It decides new-or-evidence, asks what caught your eye, hands to `seed-capture` |

## Where an idea stands

Plain words: **new · linked · used · gone quiet · unlinked · retired.**
Everything in `session/` says `seedling` today because nothing has ever moved. That is
the starting line, not a problem.

## What it never does

Decide. Delete. Capture new ideas (that is `seed-capture`). Speak first. Change your
words. Rename anything. Write outside the seedbank folder.

## Every answer has two parts

The answer to what you asked, then a few lines on what it noticed in passing. If you
keep ignoring the second part, it gets shorter.
