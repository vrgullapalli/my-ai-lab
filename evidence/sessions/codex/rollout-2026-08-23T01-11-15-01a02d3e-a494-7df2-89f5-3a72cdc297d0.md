## What should “Signal” mean?

For this system, I would define a Signal as:

> **A traceable observation, behavior, change, tension, contradiction, commitment, or outcome that could materially change what we believe, prioritize, create, distribute, productize, or revise.**

That is broader than the existing Seed skill’s definition of a qualified Signal as evidence of a “consequential decision tension.”

Consequential decision tensions remain especially important because they connect directly to your market position. But they are only one kind of useful Signal inside the engine.

## The Signal model we actually need

Signals need two dimensions.

### 1. What happened?

Use the existing families:

- Decision pressure
- Friction
- Workaround
- Commitment
- Consequence
- Change

These describe the evidence itself.

### 2. What could the evidence change?

I recommend five Signal classes:

1. **Problem and market signals**  
   Evidence that accountable leaders face conflicting explanations, unclear reality, or consequential decisions.

2. **Audience and use signals**  
   Someone applies an artifact, changes a question, forwards it, requests evidence, rejects it, or uses it in a decision.

3. **Intellectual-asset signals**  
   A distinction, method, diagnostic, explanation, or tool proves reusable across situations—or people begin requesting it by name.

4. **Productization signals**  
   Repeated pull, willingness to pay, requests for guided application, recurring customization, or a problem that cannot be solved by content alone.

5. **System and strategy signals**  
   Production friction, weak evidence, AI overreach, failed anonymization, non-use, a broken workflow, or counterevidence to the charter’s assumptions.

This prevents “Signal” from becoming synonymous with “possible content topic.”

## Where Seeds belong

A Seed should be a provisional record state, not the entire sensing system:

```text
Source or experience
        ↓
Observation
        ↓
Signal candidate / Seed
        ↓
Qualification
        ↓
Qualified Signal
        ↓
Decision about what it informs
        ├── Problem hypothesis
        ├── Intellectual-asset candidate
        ├── Content or distribution change
        ├── Product opportunity
        ├── System correction
        └── Strategy-change proposal
```

A qualified Signal does not automatically become an intellectual asset or content. It changes a decision about what deserves further work.

## What is limiting about the current skills?

The repository’s [seed-capture skill](/Users/venkatgullapalli/Documents/my-ai-lab-v2/chief-of-staff/.claude/skills/seed-capture/SKILL.md) asks whether something is repeated, belongs to you, contains a portable claim, or could be requested by name.

That makes it useful for capturing:

- Venkat-originated language
- Named ideas
- Beliefs and distinctions
- Potential intellectual property
- Memorable rules

But it can miss:

- A single consequential contradiction
- An unexpected audience reaction
- An artifact that nobody uses
- A referrer who explains your value differently
- A workflow failure
- Evidence that undermines a current assumption
- A behavior change with no memorable phrase attached

The newer [seed skill](/Users/venkatgullapalli/.codex/skills/seed/SKILL.md) is a stronger foundation because its capture threshold is deliberately low. It accepts observations, questions, hypotheses, and contradictions without requiring repetition or proof.

Its Signal qualification standard, however, is optimized for consequential decision tensions. That should remain one qualification lane—not the definition of every Signal the Content Foundry needs.

## My recommendation

Rename the engine’s front door conceptually:

> **Signal Intake and Qualification**

Within it, “Seed” means only:

> An early, traceable candidate that has been preserved but not yet qualified.

Keep the strongest protections from both skills:

- Exact source and attribution
- Observation separated from interpretation
- Internal evidence not presented as market evidence
- Contradictions preserved
- Zero captures is acceptable
- No automatic promotion
- No automatic conversion into content, products, rules, or strategy changes

The important inversion is this:

> The purpose of capture is not to find more things to publish. It is to discover what should change—and whether content is even the right response.

For example, your statement that the engine “feels like vaporware” is not merely a content Seed. It is a **system Signal candidate**:

- Observed event: the conceptual architecture could not produce a visible operating experience.
- Signal families: friction and consequence.
- Possible implication: validate a complete manual cycle before developing more architecture.
- Possible future intellectual asset: a “vaporware test” for operating systems—but only if subsequent evidence demonstrates that the distinction is reusable.

The concrete next step is to define this five-class **Signal Intake and Qualification model** as the front end of the Intellectual Asset and Content Engine, with Seed retained as its provisional capture state—not as the boundary of what the engine can notice.
