Your CoS / Shared / Foundry suggestion identifies the right audiences, but there is a better structure:

**Organize files physically by what they are and who owns them. Use CoS, Shared, Personal Brand, and Foundries as routing views—not as storage buckets.**

### Why

The live `reference/` tree contains roughly 730 files excluding protected recordings:

- 315 third-party transcripts
- 174 creator/Venkat source files
- 108 historical handoffs
- 60 estate-discovery files
- Research reports, pilot packets, governed history, and loose files

That is not one kind of material. The root description still calls it only “frozen source material” ([README.md](/Users/venkatgullapalli/Documents/my-ai-lab-v2/chief-of-staff/README.md:22)), while D-133 also makes it Codex’s advisory report tray ([DECISIONS.md](/Users/venkatgullapalli/Documents/my-ai-lab-v2/chief-of-staff/DECISIONS.md:888)).

| Approach | Judgment |
|---|---|
| Physical `CoS/Shared/Foundries/` folders | Easy initially, but the same source may serve several systems. It encourages duplication and turns “Shared” into miscellaneous storage. |
| Physical organization only by source/lifecycle | Preserves ownership and provenance, but is weaker for everyday discovery. |
| **Source/lifecycle folders + routing views** | **Recommended.** One canonical file, several systems can find it through links. |

### Recommended model

Keep the major physical collections close to their present meaning:

```text
reference/
  README.md
  about/                 # people and creator source collections
  transcripts/           # third-party transcript corpora
  handoffs/              # historical transfers and conversations
  reports/               # synthesized/advisory work
  estate/                # discovery indexes and estate evidence
  governed-history/      # VOID, superseded material—eventually
  routes/
    chief-of-staff.md
    personal-brand.md
    shared.md
    content-foundry.md    # only when that route is active
```

Each route would contain links—not copies—with:

- Canonical owner
- Source type
- Current status
- Who may use it
- When to load it
- Quoting/privacy restrictions
- Freshness date
- What it derives from or supersedes

“Shared” should remain a small catalog and coordination layer. It should not own everything useful to multiple systems.

### Where personal-brand work belongs

Use two connected homes:

```text
work/personal-brand/
  voice/
  positioning/
  content-strategy/
  website/
```

This holds active work and drafts. Existing formal plan records should remain under `work/plans/` and be linked from the personal-brand hub.

The durable evidence stays source-owned under [reference/about/about-venkat](/Users/venkatgullapalli/Documents/my-ai-lab-v2/chief-of-staff/reference/about/about-venkat), with a personal-brand route pointing to it. The Content Foundry consumes a bounded view; it should not own or duplicate your identity evidence.

The scan found substantial related work: raw voice, voice methodology, voice operational model, voice reuse audit, positioning discovery/recovery, content-strategy readiness, `work/content/`, and the Venkat positioning evidence. These should be connected through the personal-brand hub rather than indiscriminately moved.
