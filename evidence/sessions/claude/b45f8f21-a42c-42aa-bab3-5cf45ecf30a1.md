> 
> Inside pharma: struggle to connect customers, channels, data, definitions, and journeys.
> 
> Inside platforms: rapid progress toward AI interpreting evidence and recommending action.
>

> 
> Platform intelligence is advancing faster than organizational context is becoming coherent.
> 
> That gap matters.
> 
> A Google recommendation might be excellent for Google.
> 
> A Veeva recommendation might be excellent based on CRM context.

> An agency model might be excellent based on media data.
