> ## Required structure for every finding
> 
> Use this exact structure:
> 
> ### Finding
> 
> **What it means**
> Plain-language explanation of the behavior.
> 
> **Evidence status**
> One or more of:
> `OBSERVED`
> `BEHAVIORALLY_INFERRED`
> `EXPLICIT_PREFERENCE`
> `SUPPORTED`
> `CONTEXTUAL`
> `HYPOTHESIS`
> `UNKNOWN`
> 
> **Scope/context**
> Where the finding applies and where it should not be assumed.
> 
> **Example A: observed/raw behavior**
> A short example closest to direct observed behavior or Venkat's own language.
> 
> **Example B: usable written expression**
> A short example showing how the same behavior should appear in finished writing.
> 
> **Anti-example**
> A believable version that violates the finding.
> 
> **Concrete application**
> Specific instructions for what an AI should do differently because this finding exists.
> 
> **Dial up when**
> Contexts where the behavior should become more visible.
> 
> **Dial down when**
> Contexts where restraint is preferable.
> 
> **Failure mode**
> How the behavior becomes exaggerated, fake, repetitive, sanitized, or caricatured.
> 
> **Provenance**
> Finding IDs and source references.
> 
> Do not omit the two examples.
> 
> Do not make both examples stylistically identical.
> 
> Example A should show the underlying observed behavior.
> 
> Example B should show its written translation.
>
