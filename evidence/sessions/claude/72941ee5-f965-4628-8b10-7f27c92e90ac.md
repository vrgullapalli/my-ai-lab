- Fan-out move: **enumerate every way to move each variable**, including the ones that look stupid.
- Output: raise real cost — count it, date it, name who pays. Lower friction — pre-build the artifact, shrink the decision, remove an approval step. Now it's a list of moves instead of a principle.

- Narrows to: what happens after it works.

## The answer to your question

Persuasion stacking **supplies** a clock. The honest version doesn't supply one — it **finds** the clock the reader is already on and shows it to them.
