> - relationship matters

>

> 
> Separate what is STABLE IN SENSIBILITY from what is CONTEXTUAL IN EXPRESSION.
> 
> For example:
>

> "Set the reader up before asking them to accept the point."
> 
> Its expression could differ:
> - LinkedIn: lived moment or observation first
> - argued writing: gently push on the premise first
> - executive writing: decision and consequence first
> - casual message: little setup may be needed because shared context already exists

> The underlying sensibility is stable.

> - don't invent evidence

> - "Maybe... Maybe..."
> - fragments
> - parentheses
> - starting with "But"
> - "Deeper idea:"
> - one-line landings
