SIGNAL
- What happened?
- How good is the evidence?
- What is the obvious explanation?

NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS
- KEY INSIGHT 1
- KEY INSIGHT 2
- KEY INSIGHT 3
- KEY INSIGHT 4
Use a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 4 insights, but include only insights that are genuinely high value. Do not fill the quota. Apply whichever non-obvious reasoning methods best fit the signal, such as inversion, first principles, second-order effects, counterfactuals, constraint analysis, asymmetry, option value, adjacent-possible thinking, causal decomposition, and hidden-dependency analysis. Each insight should explain what is easy to miss, why it matters, and how it changes our understanding of the problem, need, buyer, decision, or opportunity.

VALUE
- FAST VALUE: What can Venkat give someone in under two minutes that helps them see, test, decide, or act differently right now? It should be small, useful, practical, and rooted in Venkat's way of thinking rather than generic advice.
- DEEPER ASSET: If the fast value reveals a real problem or need, what next job becomes visible? Identify the most useful next framework, diagnostic, instrument, skill, workflow, or product only if the evidence supports it. Do not force productization.
- LEARNING QUESTION: What uncertainty, if resolved through real-world use or conversation, would most change our understanding, positioning, audience model, product direction, or decision? Avoid weak questions such as 'Do people like this?'
- LONG-TERM VALUE: If the signal keeps proving durable, what higher-leverage capability, IP, product, service, or business asset could emerge? Show the most credible compounding path from small immediate value to evidence, deeper understanding, stronger capability, and further learning. Do not turn this into ordinary content repurposing.

DECISION
- What changed in our beliefs?
- Recommended state: choose exactly one of IGNORE, MONITOR, INVESTIGATE, TALK, TEST, DEVELOP, or ACT.
- Decision-break evidence: what evidence would make that state wrong, premature, or unnecessary? Do not recommend TEST, DEVELOP, or ACT without naming it. DEVELOP needs repeated or direct buyer evidence, a concrete use moment, a plausible user job, and a specific reason the asset or offer should exist now.

For each signal, explicitly connect it to the relevant problem hypothesis, need hypothesis, audience and accountability condition, intellectual territory, and current business opportunity when applicable. Include counterevidence, and say 'nothing changed' when the signal does not materially update the model.
