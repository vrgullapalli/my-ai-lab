## What it does

Exactly five signals a day that could change how Venkat thinks, advises, creates, or acts
around AI, customer data, and commercial pharma. Each signal gets eight fixed parts: Signal,
Evidence, Why It Matters, Interpretation, Connection, Pattern, Practical Use, Watch Next.
The brief ends with four short synthesis sections. Venkat wrote the structure on 2026-09-09
and it is reproduced in `prompt.md` almost word for word.

Two rules make this brief different from the Market Signal Brief:
- It always produces five signals. The other brief may correctly produce zero.
- Every connection has to pass a test: what in the evidence makes this connection real. If
  that cannot be answered, the connection is left out and its heading is not printed.

## Overlap with the Market Signal Brief, on purpose

`market-signal-brief/` runs Monday to Friday at 7:00 AM and reads the same territory. The two
are not duplicates: that one hunts for evidence that moves a belief and may return nothing,
this one always returns five signals in a fixed shape with a decision state on each. This
routine reads the most recent Market Signal Brief at the start of every run so it can say what
is new rather than repeating it. If the pair turns out to be redundant in practice, retire one.
Flagged to Venkat on 2026-09-09.

## Files in this folder

- `prompt.md`: the task body. Edit this to change what the routine does.
- `runs/`: one record per run Alfred inspected (what happened, what it proved, where the full text is).
- `outputs/`: copies of published briefs pulled into the lab, one file per date.
- `build/full-prompt.md`: the complete prompt as it runs in the cloud. Generated by `../assemble.sh`. Do not edit.
- `build/routine-body.json`: the exact update body for the RemoteTrigger tool. Generated. Do not edit.

## To change this routine

1. Edit `prompt.md` (or a file in `../_shared/` if the change is shared).
2. Run `../assemble.sh` and read `build/full-prompt.md`.
3. Update the routine: RemoteTrigger `action: update`, `trigger_id: trig_01UvA4mcdFnJwFx2LM1gQrMK`, `body` from `build/routine-body.json`. Or paste the prompt on the routine page.
4. Add a dated line to the log below.
