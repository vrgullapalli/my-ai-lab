## Two small misses, both fixed the same day

- It widened the search window back to 2026-07-10 and said so, but the prompt had only allowed
  seven days. The honest fix was to widen the rule, not scold the run: pharma commercial is thin
  daily. The prompt now says start at two days, widen up to about eight weeks, and say how far
  back you went.
- Signal five gave 'Confidence: Low to Medium'. Venkat's structure asks for exactly one of Low,
  Medium, or High. The prompt now forbids ranges and says pick the lower one.
