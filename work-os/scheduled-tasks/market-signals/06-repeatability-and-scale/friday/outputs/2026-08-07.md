- **What would make us wrong:** Spain ramping faster than Germany, or results holding across Doximity's programs.

- Indegene giving Spain's ramp time against the three to four quarters.

- **LONG-TERM VALUE.** If early expansion keeps showing up, a simple bar for when a capability has earned the right to move. Real cases first.
