## Non-obvious analysis

**KEY INSIGHT: More agents per use case, not fewer.**
IQVIA went from 3.0 agents per use case in May to about 3.3 in July. With real reuse, that number should fall. "Deployed" is not defined, so this is a weak inference, but a fair question.

**KEY INSIGHT: The vendor keeps the shared core. The client's local layer gets redone each time.**
Indegene split the expert layer from the technical layer. Inizio paired one global partner with "deep local expertise." Both sell the core. Local rules, reviews, definitions, and language sit in the other layer and likely get rebuilt for each client or market. Inferred. That is where an accountable leader's scaling cost may actually sit.

**KEY INSIGHT: Ramp time is the test.**
Indegene says outcome deals take "three to four quarters to ramp up." If the capability were truly repeatable, that should shrink with each client. Nobody reported that. It is a clean number to watch.

## Challenge the interpretation

- **What else could explain this?** Vendors always call offers repeatable on investor calls. The "95 countries" case may be one tool reaching many markets through the vendor's own data.
- **Counterevidence:** Indegene's Tectonic client "expanded into a further region during the quarter." One real market move. What changed is unknown.
- **What would make us wrong:** later clients going live faster and cheaper with results holding, or a drugmaker saying a vendor tool worked the same across brands with little local work.

## Trigger

- A vendor disclosing ramp time or setup cost falling across clients.
- IQVIA or its client saying what stayed fixed and what was set up locally across the 95 countries.
- Indegene's agency-of-record proof of concept signing a next phase, with what was reused.
- A drugmaker naming a commercial AI result that held in a second brand or market.

## Value

- **FAST VALUE.** Three questions for any leader hearing "it's repeatable" from a vendor: "What did you reuse unchanged for your last client? What did you rebuild? Did ramp time drop from your first client to your fifth?"
- **DEEPER ASSET.** Not yet. Sizing the local layer needs real client cases first.
- **LEARNING QUESTION.** When a drugmaker takes a vendor capability to a second brand or market, who does the local work, and how long does it take? If the client's own teams do most of it, vendor reuse does not lower the client's scaling cost.
- **LONG-TERM VALUE.** If the local layer keeps proving to be the real cost, Venkat could help leaders size it before they buy. Real second-deployment stories come first.

## Decision

- **What changed?** We believe more strongly that vendors are building the reusable part of commercial AI, with the local layer kept separate. We dropped the worry that experience does not help.
- **Recommended state: INVESTIGATE.**
- **Why this state?** Four independent sources, and a specific, answerable question: where does the local work land?
- **What moves it next?** Any vendor or client number on second-deployment effort would move it to TALK.
