## Signal 1: Many deploy agents. Few scale them. Process is the wall.

**What happened**
- Observed, survey: Deloitte, August 12. "More than 4 in 10 leaders surveyed say their organizations have tested or deployed AI agents (42%)." But "Only 15% say they have scaled, orchestrated, multi-agent adoption in place." And "Only 5% of organizations say their business processes are highly prepared for AI agents" ([PR Newswire, August 12](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ai-agents-are-only-the-beginning-deloitte-survey-examines-the-ai-readiness-gap-and-reveals-how-enterprises-can-prepare-for-agentic-success-302848848.html)).
- Same release: "Only 1 in 5 leaders surveyed say their organizations are prepared to redesign processes for autonomous, agentic operation."
- Sample: 501 senior leaders in the United States, April to June 2026, five industries including life sciences and health care. No life sciences breakout.

**Unit of analysis**
- Large United States companies across five industries. Not pharma commercial on its own.

**Evidence strength: MEDIUM. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** Fourth survey in three weeks with the same shape. This one is larger and names a cause: process readiness. Still self-reported, and not pharma-specific.

**Obvious explanation**
- A consulting firm found that clients need process redesign, which it sells.

**Hypothesis update**
- Hypothesis affected: "Customer data can be technically available while remaining operationally untrustworthy," read here as: live in one place is not spread across the work. Also the problem hypothesis, "AI deployment advances faster than the organization can define value, redesign the workflow, and prove the result." Secondary dependency: Operating Model.
- Direction: **STRENGTHENS.** The gap is now measured again, and the survey points at the workflow, not the model, as what fails to move.

**Pattern basis**
- Reinforces Caylent (August 6: 59.5% in production, 23.5% broadly deployed) and two earlier surveys. Different firms, same gap.
- New: a reason. The tool travels. The process does not. Stable, not closing.

## Non-obvious analysis

**KEY INSIGHT: The portable unit is the agent. The stuck unit is the process.**
Agents get deployed easily and scaled rarely, and only 5% say processes are ready. Inferred: the part that travels is the smallest part. Each new brand brings its own process, and that is where a rollout quietly becomes a custom project.

**KEY INSIGHT: The shared core is built on the side trusted less.**
Vendors sell one core across brands, and Dentsu centralizes. But drugmakers rate vendor AI at 27% versus 71% for their own. Inferred: the accountable leader inherits the gap between what the vendor reuses and what the team will rely on.

## Challenge the interpretation

- **What else could explain this?** Earnings season plus survey season. Vendors talk reach, consultants find readiness gaps.
- **Counterevidence:** Definitive tests portability on purpose before launch. OptimizeRx's 25% AI software growth could reflect real repeat use, but there is no per-brand number.
- **What would make us wrong:** a Deloitte life sciences breakout showing pharma closer to scaled; a vendor showing faster second-brand setup; a commercial split where drugmakers rate vendor AI as high as their own.

## Trigger

- Any vendor naming time or cost to set up a second brand compared with the first.
- Definitive reporting pilot results by customer type, drugmakers versus health systems.
- Indegene giving Spain's ramp time against the "three to four quarters" its finance chief gave July 31.
- A pharma-specific number on processes redesigned, not just agents deployed.

## Value

- **FAST VALUE.** One question for a leader asked to scale an agent: "When this moves to the next brand, does the process move with it, or does the next team rebuild it?" If they rebuild, you are buying repeated custom work.
- **DEEPER ASSET.** Not yet. No second-deployment numbers from anyone.
- **LEARNING QUESTION.** In pharma commercial, is the process that blocks scale the same across brands, such as medical and legal review, or different each time?
- **LONG-TERM VALUE.** If the process keeps proving non-portable, a simple way to split a capability into what travels (the agent, the data feed) and what must be redesigned locally (review steps, handoffs). Real cases first.

## Decision

- **What changed?** Deployed is not scaled, now with a named reason: process readiness. And a first sign buyers trust vendor AI less than their own.
- **Recommended state: INVESTIGATE.** Same as last week.
- **Why this state?** The gap is well measured. Missing is any second setting getting cheaper or faster.
- **What moves it next?** A ramp time or setup cost for a second brand or market moves it to TALK.
