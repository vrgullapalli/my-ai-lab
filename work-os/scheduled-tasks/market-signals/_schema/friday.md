RULES

Use the daily {{PILLAR}} briefings as evidence, not as sections to summarize. Select no more than 3 to 5 weekly signals. Every selected signal must materially relate to {{PILLAR}}. Prefer fewer strong signals over more weak ones. Separate observation from interpretation. Do not count repeated coverage as independent evidence. State the unit of analysis. Connect each signal to an explicit {{PILLAR}} hypothesis. Include counterevidence. Define what would make the interpretation wrong. Define a concrete trigger for changing state. Say 'nothing changed' when warranted.
{{EXTRA_RULES}}
Do not generate content ideas. Do not force product opportunities. Do not manufacture novelty. Do not create work from weak signals. Keep it concise, plainspoken, practical, evidence-led, and non-hyped.
