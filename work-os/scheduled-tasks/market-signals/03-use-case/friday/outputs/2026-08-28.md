**Evidence**

And "AI must improve an already-healthy commercial engine, not merely coincide with its growth" ([LucidQuest, August 26](https://www.lqventures.com/lundbeck-eversana-ai-commercialization/)).
- **New Friday:** 80-plus pharma leaders, "a panel discussion with several clients including Lundbeck," and a next focus on "broader commercialization services powered by AI" ([PM360, August 28](https://pm360online.com/eversana-hosts-80-companies-together-with-google-cloud-to-showcase-whats-next-in-ai-agency-commercialization/)). No metric.

**Evidence strength: MEDIUM. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** A named buyer's spending choice, but one deal and no value shown.

**Obvious explanation:** the first phase worked, and Lundbeck keeps numbers private.

**Hypothesis update**
- Affected: "AI deployment advances faster than the organization can define value, redesign the workflow, and prove the result." Primary: Use Case. Secondary: Operating Model.
- Direction: **STRENGTHENS.** The likeliest stage for a buyer number passed without one, as far as public reports show.

**Pattern basis:** repeating. Lundbeck is the one new voice. Many outlets, one release, counted once.

## Signal 2: AI call notes spread through sales-system switches, not job-by-job choices

**What happened?** Observed: Regeneron and Biogen committed globally to Veeva's sales system, which ships with an AI call-note tool. Interpretation: the job spreads because it rides inside a bigger system decision.

**Unit of analysis:** large drugmakers' sales-system choices, and one job inside them: reps speak visit notes, the AI structures and checks them, and flags treatment barriers.

**Evidence**
- "With Vault CRM, Regeneron plans to generate Commercial Evidence from AI-powered field engagement, uncovering hidden barriers to treatment adoption" (Vault CRM is Veeva's customer relationship management system). That line is Veeva's; Regeneron's only quoted voice is its chief digital and technology officer ([PR Newswire, August 25](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/veeva-vault-crm-selected-by-regeneron-302858885.html)).
- Biogen committed globally, quoted through its global chief information officer ([PR Newswire, August 25](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/veeva-vault-crm-selected-by-biogen-302858894.html)). No go-live date in either.
- "A top 20 biopharma deployed Vault CRM and the Agentic Call Report across its full U.S. field team in the quarter" (the same system) ([PR Newswire, August 26](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/veeva-announces-fiscal-2027-second-quarter-results-302860933.html)). Seller claim, no outcome.
- **New Thursday:** 12 of the top 20 drugmakers are now committed to it. On rival Salesforce's customers, Veeva's chief executive "said some large projects are facing delays and implementation problems" ([Zacks via Yahoo Finance, August 27](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/veev-q2-earnings-call-centers-143700367.html)). A rival's word.

**Evidence strength: MEDIUM. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** Two named buyers and a full-team claim. But these count signatures, not use or barriers fixed.

**Obvious explanation:** drugmakers switch systems for their own reasons and take the AI feature along.

**Hypothesis update**
- Affected: the belief "measures stop where the AI's part ends," and the accountability condition. Primary: Use Case. Secondary: Operating Model.
- Direction: **COMPLICATES.** The spread is real and named. But the job is not chosen on its own, so its value may never be counted on its own.

**Pattern basis:** strengthening in spread, flat in proof. Faded August 21, back August 26, now with named buyers.

## Signal 3: Doctors ask AI first, and marketers start to follow

**What happened?** Observed: seller data says most U.S. doctors use AI tools for clinical information, and few check the answers. Interpretation: the job shifts from delivering content to making sure the answer a doctor gets is right.

**Unit of analysis:** U.S. doctors' information habits, as reported by sellers to pharma marketers.

**Evidence**
- From 896 surveyed doctors plus email and ad data: "74 percent of physicians use at least one AI tool for clinical information," "only 27 percent say they rigorously verify what those tools return," and "ChatGPT accounts for 97 percent of the referrals measured" ([HealthLink Dimensions and DeepIntent release, National Law Review, August 26](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/new-research-shows-74-percent-physicians-use-ai-clinical-information)).
- "Pharma marketers are starting to shift their strategy, so far focusing most changes around answer engine optimization" ([BioPharma Dive, sponsored by PharmaForceIQ, August 24](https://www.biopharmadive.com/spons/pharma-marketers-need-a-share-of-answer-strategy-before-ai-spend/828227/)). No drugmaker named.
- The same sponsor launched a tool to "Route recommended changes to the right workflow stage and owner for approval" ([PR Newswire, August 26](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pharmaforceiq-launches-optichannel-360-the-first-ai-native-answer-to-action-engine-for-pharma-commercial-teams-302860532.html)). Not independent, but it targets last week's gap: acting on AI's output.

**Evidence strength: LOW. EARLY INDICATOR.** Two sellers, one survey. No drugmaker says it changed a plan.

**Obvious explanation:** marketing sellers have a new service to sell.

**Hypothesis update**
- Affected: the need hypothesis that teams need shared definitions and provenance "before more automation raises the cost of inconsistency." Primary: Use Case. Secondary: Meaning and Context; Trust and Control.
- Direction: **STRENGTHENS**, narrowly. If doctors rarely check AI answers, a wrong brand fact costs more.

**Pattern basis:** emerging. "No pattern" on Wednesday; now a survey and a launch, both from sellers.

## Non-obvious analysis

**KEY INSIGHT: The person who signs is not the person who counts**
- Regeneron's and Biogen's voices were technology chiefs. Reps take the notes. The value, a barrier fixed, lands with access or brand teams. Observed for the first two; inferred for the third.
- Why it matters: no one in commercial is asked to set a baseline. The accountable leader inherits a use case nobody agreed to measure.

**KEY INSIGHT: Numbers show up where the job is a bounded check and a person keeps the hard cases**
- Tufts modeled AI trial monitoring at "up to $21 million" for one late-stage program, while the clinical research associate "is still involved in checking to ensure the data makes sense" ([PharmaVoice, August 26](https://www.pharmavoice.com/news/ai-finally-proving-worth-drug-rd-tufts/828783/)). Modeled, cancer trials only, built with vendor data.
- Cigna's benefit routing: "50 times faster," and "Human reviewers remain accountable for oversight and complex case handling" ([Autonomize case study, August 26](https://autonomize.ai/insights/enhancing-the-pharmacy-experience-for-providers-and-patients-with-a-faster-more-predictable-path-to-care/)). Vendor page, no baseline.
- Why it matters: both are narrow checks with one owner. Commercial jobs being scaled hand their value to someone else's step. Inferred: the missing number may be built into the job, not shyness.

- **What else could explain this?** The silence is not pharma's alone. McKinsey: "Organizations' conviction in AI is growing faster than the immediate financial returns they can attribute to it" ([The Register, August 25](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/25/mckinsey-says-enterprise-ai-is-finally-on-the-road-to-roi/5292388)). Earnings week may explain the cluster.
- **Counterevidence:** Lundbeck spent more after a first phase, a buyer judgment even without a number.
- **What would make us wrong:** a named drugmaker publishing a before and after for one commercial AI job.

## Value

- **FAST VALUE:** Three names. For any commercial AI deal: who signed it, who does the daily work, and who owns the number that should move? If the third is missing, or has no baseline from before go-live, the use case can't be proven later.
- **DEEPER ASSET:** not yet. At most a one-page "baseline before scale" sheet, if a leader asks.
- **LEARNING QUESTION:** When the AI note tool comes with a new sales system, does anyone in commercial own acting on a flagged barrier, and was a baseline set first?
- **LONG-TERM VALUE:** if buyers keep scaling without measures: the three-names question, then a pre-scale check, then one company's flagged barriers read against outcomes, then an outside baseline at each platform switch.
