## Signal 1: Patient support names one job for AI: spot who will stop, then reach them

**What happened?** Two patient support sellers named the same job: predict which patients will stop a drug, and act first. One gave an outcome number. Neither named a drugmaker or split credit between the model and the people who make the calls. Observed.

**Unit of analysis:** sellers of patient support services (hubs and pharmacies) to drugmakers. The job belongs to hub and pharmacy staff.

**Evidence**
- Adheris (until now MedAdvisor Solutions), August 14: "returning approximately 65% of patients to therapy and driving more than 15.8 million incremental refills for clients in recent years" ([Pharmaceutical Executive, August 14](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/medadvisor-solutions-rebrands-adheris)). Claimed. No method, baseline, time frame, or client. Its own release two days earlier says it uses "predictive analytics to identify and act on the moments that matter most" ([PRWeb, August 12](https://www.prweb.com/releases/medadvisor-solutions-is-now-adheris-company-returns-to-its-original-name-and-sharpens-focus-on-innovating-patient-engagement-302849325.html)). Same source, not independent.
- Cardinal Health's Sonexus, sponsored post, August 14: "improving call quality and predicting non-adherence," with governance built by "our AI Center of Excellence" and the business ([Drug Channels, sponsored, August 14](https://www.drugchannels.net/2026/08/hub-transitions-need-proven-plans-smart.html)). Claimed. No number tied to AI.
- **Contrast, new Friday:** a rare disease pharmacy credits outcomes to people: "Patient education, counseling, and support continue long after the package lands on the patient's doorstep." No AI is mentioned ([Drug Channels, sponsored by PANTHERx Rare, August 21](https://www.drugchannels.net/2026/08/invisible-work-of-rare-pharmacy.html)). Observed.
- Background, July 30: Infinitus launched an AI-first hub where "Human experts engage where judgment or empathy is needed," and AI handles routine enrollment and status calls. It claims it "absorbed a 144% patient volume surge with zero additional full-time employees hired" ([PR Newswire, July 30](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/infinitus-launches-ai-first-human-backed-hub-solution-to-replace-specialty-pharmas-legacy-call-center-infrastructure-302838311.html)). Claimed. No drugmaker named.

**Evidence strength: LOW. EARLY INDICATOR.** Two sellers: a rebrand story and a sponsored post. Real use is claimed, not shown.

**Obvious explanation:** sellers are putting an AI label on old refill reminder programs.

**Hypothesis update**
- Affected: "AI deployment advances faster than the organization can define value, redesign the workflow, and prove the result." Primary: Use Case. Secondary: Operating Model, since the outcome depends on who makes the call and how fast.
- Direction: **COMPLICATES.** Last week the measure stopped at the AI's output. Here it reaches the outcome, refills, but mixes the model's work with the people's, so no one can say what the prediction added.

**Pattern basis:** emerging, too early to tell. Two companies, one job, one week. The rare pharmacy post contradicts the credit, not the job. Adheris's 65% (patients returned) and Veeva's 65% (notes flagging a barrier) are different steps. Never compare them.

## Non-obvious analysis

**KEY INSIGHT: Two ways a number can miss the use case**
- Last week, Veeva's number stopped at the AI's output. This week, Adheris's number jumps to the outcome but blends the model with the hub staff. Inferred.
- Why it matters: both hide what the AI added. A buyer needs a comparison: flagged patients who got a call, against similar patients who did not. And if AI takes the routine calls, as Infinitus describes, the human job gets harder, not smaller.

**KEY INSIGHT: Sellers are widening while the proof is narrow**
- EVERSANA added several functions in one release. Axtria is hiring 1,000 engineers to make AI work inside clients. Neither published a result for one job. Observed.
- Why it matters: when sellers sell the labor to make AI work, the use case is not yet a product. It is assembled client by client. Inferred. That is where an outside check of "is this job real here?" fits.

## Challenge the interpretation

- **What else could explain this?** Refill gains may come from reminders and pharmacist calls, not prediction. Drugmaker silence may be legal caution. "Beyond pilots" may be budget season talk.
- **Counterevidence:** Adheris gave an outcome-level number. Sonexus described governance built with its business side.
- **What would make us wrong:** a drugmaker or hub publishing results for flagged patients against a comparison group.

## Trigger

- A drugmaker names an adherence prediction program with a before and after, or Adheris says how "incremental refills" is counted.
- EVERSANA names a client or a result at its August 27 event.
- A drug company names its measure for any commercial AI job: move to TALK. One more week of seller-only numbers: move to MONITOR.

## Value

- **FAST VALUE:** "Who would have called anyway?" For any AI adherence claim, ask: who got flagged, who acted, and what happened to similar people nobody flagged? If the third answer is missing, the claim measures the program, not the AI.
- **DEEPER ASSET:** not yet. Two seller claims do not justify a tool.
- **LEARNING QUESTION:** When a hub model flags a patient as likely to stop, how many get a human call in time, and do they stay on therapy more than similar patients not flagged?
- **LONG-TERM VALUE:** if adherence becomes the first job with outcome numbers, the credit split becomes the buyer's question: first in conversations, then one hub's data read through it, then a check before renewing a patient support AI contract.

## Decision

- **What changed?** Two sellers now name one narrow job, catching patients before they stop. Its proof mixes AI and human work. Everything else held.
- **Recommended state: INVESTIGATE.**
- **Why this state?** The question is open and cheap to work, and the new job sharpens it. Neither the TALK nor the MONITOR bar was met.
- **What moves it next?** A buyer naming a measure moves it to TALK. One more quiet week moves it to MONITOR.
