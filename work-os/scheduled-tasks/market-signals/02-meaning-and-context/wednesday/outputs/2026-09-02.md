## Pattern 1: Someone has to own the meaning

**What appears to be changing**
- Observed: a buyer, a standards body, and a vendor each describe a named person or group who writes, approves, or tests definitions. Inferred: the market is moving from "have one definition" to "who stands behind it."

**Evidence so far**
- Agios Pharmaceuticals, in a Tellius case study, September 1. Deepanshu Arora, Commercial Analytics Leader: "We wanted more control on the semantic layer and to build the business context into it." And: "Different terms mean different things at different organizations." And: "Do not assume the AI will automatically know your organization. You have to train it like a new employee" ([Tellius](https://www.tellius.com/resources/blog/how-agios-pharmaceuticals-runs-ai-native-commercial-analytics-at-launch-scale)). AI here means artificial intelligence. A buyer's words, published by his vendor. Claimed.
- Komodo Health and the National Committee for Quality Assurance, announced August 26 (missed last week), covered August 31. "Historically, quantitatively testing a new HEDIS measure has taken two to three months. With Komodo's capabilities, NCQA tested the COF-E measure in about a month" ([Komodo press release](https://www.komodohealth.com/press/ncqa-accelerating-the-future-of-healthcare-quality-measurement/)). HEDIS is the Healthcare Effectiveness Data and Information Set, the measures health plans report. NCQA is the committee. COF-E is a colonoscopy follow-up measure. The speed figure is the vendor's claim.
- From the same release, Tricia Elliott, Vice President, Quality Implementation at the committee: "The future of quality measurement depends on our ability to ensure measures remain meaningful, actionable, and aligned with how care is delivered." Coverage says the goal is to "make quality measures more responsive to changes in clinical practice" ([HLTH, August 31](https://hlth.com/insights/news/komodo-health-and-ncqa-partner-to-advance-healthcare-quality-measurement); also [Health Care Innovation, August 31](https://www.hcinnovationgroup.com/analytics-ai/quality-continuous-performance-improvement/news/55402002/ncqa-works-with-komodo-to-accelerate-quality-measure-development)).
- Boomi, September 2, Agent Control Plane: it grounds reasoning "in expert-endorsed business definitions and end-to-end data lineage" ([Boomi press release](https://via.ritzau.dk/pressemeddelelse/15131008/boomi-delivers-the-critical-infrastructure-that-brings-control-to-enterprise-ai?publisherId=90456&lang=en)). It does not say who the experts are. Claimed.
- Background, late August (exact day not shown): Eli Lilly posted a director role for its United States commercial technology team to "Define data platform strategy, semantic layer, ontologies, knowledge graphs, and reference architectures," aligned to its "HCP/HCO data governance," where HCP and HCO mean healthcare professional and healthcare organization ([LinkedIn job posting](https://in.linkedin.com/jobs/view/director-enterprise-data-analytics-architect-lilly-usa-commercial-technology-at-eli-lilly-and-company-4458171781)). Observed posting.
- Contrast, Microsoft, August 28: new documentation for its data agent says descriptions "give the data agent business context about tables, columns, and other elements." But: "By default, the data agent inherits available descriptions from the data source" ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/data-science/data-agent-schema-object-descriptions)). Preview only. No owner or review step described.

**Unit of analysis**
- One biotech commercial analytics team, one health quality standards body, one integration vendor, one pharma job posting. Not a market-wide claim.

**Hypothesis affected**
- "Teams need shared definitions, ownership, provenance, and repeatability before more automation raises the cost of inconsistency." Also Continuity of Meaning. Secondary: Operating Model, who owns the fit check.
- **Direction: STRENGTHENS.** A pharma analytics leader says meaning is local and must be built in by his team. A standards body shows what an owner with a review cycle looks like. Friday said nothing checks whether a definition still fits. Too broad: where a clear owner exists, as in health quality measures, the fit check exists and is getting faster. Inferred.

**Evidence strength: MEDIUM. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** Four separate organizations, three kinds of evidence: a buyer quote, a standards process with a time result, and a hiring move. Held back from HIGH because every item is self-reported, and none shows an owner catching a stale definition.

**Pattern basis**
- Reinforcing: Friday's fit question. Paul Wachtler, InformationWeek, August 28: "A definition approved at the start of a quarter may no longer fit after the forecast changes or leadership shifts the decision it is trying to make" ([InformationWeek](https://www.informationweek.com/machine-learning-ai/why-ai-analysts-give-confident-answers-to-the-wrong-questions)). Already counted Friday.
- Contradicting: Microsoft's default carries meaning forward with no one approving it.
- Status: Strengthening.

**Challenge**
- Simplest read: vendors are writing buyer quotes and feature lists that say what buyers want to hear.
- What else: the Komodo story may be a data access story, a Foundation gain, not a meaning one.
- Wrong if: the committee's faster loop only tests new measures and never revises old ones. Or if Agios' "control" means access rights, not definitions.

**Next evidence before Friday**
- A second pharma commercial team naming who approves how key measures are defined.
- The committee naming a measure revised or retired because practice changed.

**State: WAIT FOR FRIDAY.** Friday's planned question to a pharma analytics leader already covers this, and the Agios quote gives it a real example.

## Pattern 2: Business rules stay in the systems; the agent calls them

**What appears to be changing**
- Observed: more vendors advise that agents should call existing systems for business logic, not rebuild it. Inferred: the split between system logic and model judgment is becoming standard advice.

**Evidence so far**
- Anthropic, September 2, guide to commerce agents. Existing systems each encode "logic tuned over years and seeing signals the model never will." And: "The agent's tools should call those systems, not reimplement them, and the tool boundary is where their logic ends and the model's judgment takes over" ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/the-anatomy-of-effective-commerce-agents)). Advice, not a result.
- ACTO, a pharma field AI vendor, chief executive Parth Khanna, Pharmaceutical Executive, September 1: "When agents are designed around a specific role, they can inherit the compliance protocols and guardrails that correspond to that role" ([PharmExec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/why-ai-trust-comes-down-one-equation-parth-khanna)). A vendor's view.
- Boomi's same September 2 launch makes agent actions "policy-governed" ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/data-technologies/news/366649690/Boomi-intros-layer-for-controlling-AI-costs-and-connections)). Same event as Pattern 1. Counted once.
- Background: Salesforce with Anthropic, and Databricks, August 27, counted Friday.

**Unit of analysis**
- Vendor guidance for agents, one in pharma field work. Not a deployment.

**Hypothesis affected**
- Human-Governed Agentic Operating Systems, and his probabilistic-versus-deterministic principle. Secondary: Trust and Control.
- **Direction: COMPLICATES.** Anthropic's line is close to his principle word for word. That makes the split common ground, not his edge. And "where their logic ends and the model's judgment takes over" is exactly where meaning slips: the model still picks which tool and which rule fit the question. Inferred.

**Evidence strength: LOW. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** Advice and claims only. Anthropic appears twice across two weeks, once as Salesforce's partner and once on its own.

**Pattern basis**
- Reinforcing: five vendors in two weeks, of four kinds. Contradicting: none found. Status: Repeating.

**Challenge**
- Simplest read: vendors protecting their place by owning the rules.
- Wrong if: pilot errors come mostly from bad data or broken tools, not from the model picking the wrong rule.

**Next evidence before Friday**
- A named life sciences customer running rules as tools.
- A case where an agent called the right tool with the wrong meaning, such as the wrong period or customer type.

**State: WAIT FOR FRIDAY.** A repeat with no customer.

## Interesting signal. No pattern yet: "context layer" stretched to mean a unified record

- Salesforce, September 2, customer award story: Tottenham Hotspur used its data product "to consolidate disparate data sources into a single, real-time fan context layer, unifying more than 4.6 million fans and 30 disparate systems into a single 'golden record'" ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/customer-success-award-winners-2026/)). A golden record is unified data. The story says nothing about shared meaning.
- Salesforce, August 31, Winter '27 release: "Tableau Knowledge unlocks trapped enterprise data by automatically ingesting structured and unstructured data, generating living knowledge graphs, and delivering curated context to agents via open standards" ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/winter-2027-product-release-announcement/)). No availability date. Claimed.
- Analysts, TechTarget, August 31. David Menninger, ISG Software Research: "We still don't know all the information that an enterprise collects and what it means." Donald Farmer, TreeHive Strategy: "Tableau is right to focus on context, the one thing AI models do not supply" ([TechTarget](https://www.techtarget.com/data-technologies/news/366649895/As-AI-evolves-Tableau-talks-direction-ahead-of-Dreamforce)).
- Counterweight, from people. Alexis Cogswell, Vice President, Real World Insights at Inizio Medical, an agency serving pharma, August 28: real-world data insight "is only truly valuable if it is contextualized and validated by SMEs and HCPs with local insights," where SMEs are subject matter experts and HCPs are healthcare professionals ([Pharmaceutical Commerce](https://www.pharmaceuticalcommerce.com/view/the-evolving-role-of-rwd-and-ai-driven-insights-in-pharma-strategy)).
- Background, August 27, missed last week. Salesforce's own employee agent: its "library of more than 1,000 internal knowledge articles was full of overlapping policies and outdated text." It "archived hundreds of outdated pages," and now "AI scoring flags articles that need work" ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/lessons-from-first-year-of-employee-agent/)). Its hard work was fixing meaning, not joining data.
- Why it may matter, inferred: unified data is not understood context. If a pharma leader hears "context layer" and pictures a golden record, the harder work stays unfunded.
- Hypothesis: "Customer data can be technically available while remaining operationally untrustworthy." **TOO EARLY TO TELL.** One vendor's wording, twice.
- Watch next: a second vendor, or a life sciences customer, calling a unified profile a context layer.
- **State: WAIT FOR FRIDAY.**
