## Signal 1: Local meaning becomes a supported feature

**SIGNAL**
- **What happened.** Observed: two platforms shipped ways to keep and reuse meaning where the work happens, before it's shared. Inferred: local meaning is now a step toward shared meaning.
- **Unit of analysis.** Two cross-industry data platforms. Not pharma.
- **Evidence.**
  - Databricks, August 20: "Local metric views are now generally available." Teams define measures and joins inside one dashboard, "without first publishing them to Unity Catalog," its shared catalog. A shared definition can also be extended "locally to add dashboard-specific measures and fields." Same day, importing "reports and data models from Power BI and Tableau" became generally available ([Databricks release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/databricks/ai-bi/release-notes/2026)). Observed.
  - Palantir, August 18: "Skills provide a first-class way to capture and share organizational knowledge," and "The new Analysis lookup tool turns past analyses into a source of reusable knowledge" ([Palantir announcements](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/announcements/2026-08)). Observed. A separate vendor.
  - Counterevidence: "without first publishing" implies a later step to share.
- **Evidence strength: LOW. EARLY INDICATOR.** Releases only. No customer, failure, or result.
- **Obvious explanation.** Speed. Analysts don't want to wait on a central data team.

**HYPOTHESIS UPDATE**
- **Affected:** "Local definitions, tagging, mappings, scripts, and ownership choices make the same data support different answers." Continuity of Meaning. Secondary: Operating Model (who promotes a local definition).
- **Direction: COMPLICATES.** We treat local definitions as the problem. Vendors now treat them as a stage. The risk moves to "nobody promotes, retires, or reconciles them." Inferred.

**PATTERN BASIS**
- Two vendors in three days, different routes, same direction. Status: Emerging.

**NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS**

**KEY INSIGHT: A local measure built on a shared one looks official.**
- A dashboard's "revenue" may be the shared definition plus a local tweak. The viewer can't tell. More local extension means a shared name can hide different math. Inferred.

**KEY INSIGHT: Old meaning now travels forward.**
- Imported reports carry the rules they were built with. Past analyses carry the definitions used then. A pharma data consultant, August 7: "Historical data reflects old system logic, not current definitions" ([Perceptive Analytics](https://www.perceptive-analytics.com/how-to-build-pharma-commercial-data-for-ai-in-2026/)). Background. Reuse without a date on the definition turns stale meaning into current context. Inferred.

**CHALLENGE THE INTERPRETATION**
- **What else:** poor workflow design, long waits on a central team. Not meaning at all.
- **Counterevidence:** local-first may replace shadow spreadsheets, which are worse.
- **Wrong if:** teams using local definitions report fewer conflicting numbers.

**TRIGGER**
- Two numbers for the same named measure traced to a local extension. Or a vendor flags which measures are local.

**VALUE**
- **Fast value:** "For each number on this dashboard, is it the shared definition, or one added just here?"
- **Deeper asset:** not yet.
- **Learning question:** in pharma commercial teams, how many local versions of a key measure exist, and who reconciles them?
- **Long-term value:** a check listing local definitions and their drift from the shared one could become a diagnostic step.

**DECISION**
- **What changed:** local meaning is a supported feature, not a workaround.
- **Recommended state: MONITOR.** Right on his hypothesis, but releases only.
- **What moves it next:** one described conflict traced to a local definition moves it to INVESTIGATE.

## Signal 2: Reviewing learned meaning. One vendor's gap

**SIGNAL**
- **What happened.** Observed: Databricks added no changelog or approval step for its ontology. AWS has people review what its graph infers. Inferred: vendors version agents faster than learned meaning.
- **Unit of analysis.** Three cross-industry platforms. Not pharma.
- **Evidence.**
  - Databricks, August 20 notes: nothing on the ontology, a changelog, or approval ([Databricks release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/databricks/ai-bi/release-notes/2026)). Staff member, August 4: "per-answer citations, but not yet a full reviewable changelog or diff." No posts since ([Databricks Community](https://community.databricks.com/t5/data-engineering/genie-ontology/td-p/164536)).
  - AWS, June 17: "Data stewards manage the graph through the AWS Management Console, reviewing inferred relationships, promoting them to production and attaching business definitions and usage rules" ([VentureBeat, June 17](https://venturebeat.com/data/aws-enters-the-context-layer-race-with-a-graph-that-learns-from-agents-not-manual-curation)). Background. Vendor description.
  - Snowflake, August 21: evaluations can target "a specific agent version," so a scheduled "evaluation stays reproducible instead of tracking a mutable live version" ([Snowflake release note](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/release-notes/2026/other/2026-08-21-cortex-agent-eval-version-targeting-ga)). Observed.
- **Evidence strength: LOW. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** Three weeks, no real case of a wrong learned definition.
- **Obvious explanation.** Preview features ship first, admin screens later.

**HYPOTHESIS UPDATE**
- **Affected:** Context Continuity, carrying context forward "without gaining invisible authority." Secondary: Trust and Control.
- **Direction: COMPLICATES.** True for Databricks. Elsewhere review is a design choice. The leader's question: "Does your platform let someone review what it learned, and does anyone?"

**PATTERN BASIS**
- Reinforcing: Databricks default-on (August 6), open to all (August 13), no changelog. Contradicting: AWS. Adds nothing: Snowflake's agent versioning.
- Status: Repeating, flat.

**NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS**

**KEY INSIGHT: Versioning the agent is not versioning its meaning.**
- The Snowflake note doesn't say whether the definitions an agent uses are part of its version. Unknown. If not, a pinned test can keep passing while the business meaning shifts. A consistent answer can be consistently wrong. Inferred.

**CHALLENGE THE INTERPRETATION**
- **What else:** unclear ownership. A review screen nobody staffs equals none.
- **Counterevidence:** per-answer citations already show what fed one answer.
- **Wrong if:** AWS stewards approve in bulk, or a Databricks changelog goes unused.

**TRIGGER**
- A wrong learned definition described, with who caught it.

**VALUE**
- **Fast value:** "When your agent's answer changes, can you tell whether the data, the model, or a definition changed?"
- **Deeper asset:** not yet.
- **Learning question:** do pharma commercial teams know whether their tool learns definitions at all?
- **Long-term value:** unchanged from last week.

**DECISION**
- **What changed:** the gap is narrower than we said two weeks running.
- **Recommended state: MONITOR.** Down from INVESTIGATE.
- **Why:** no described failure in three weeks, and not market-wide.
- **What moves it next:** a described wrong learned definition moves it to INVESTIGATE. In pharma, straight to TALK.
