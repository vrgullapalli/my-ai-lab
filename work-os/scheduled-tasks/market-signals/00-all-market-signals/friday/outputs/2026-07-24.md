## Signal 1: Software makers are paying to own the trusted-data layer under AI

**SIGNAL**
- **What happened.** Observed: SAP closed Prior Labs (July 17) and cut its profit outlook for it and a second data deal (July 23). Progress bought Domo's analytics business (July 22). Interpretation: the contest has moved to who owns governed business data.
- **Unit of analysis.** Enterprise data and AI software vendors, plus one pharma marketing vendor. Not pharma buyers.
- **Evidence.**
  - SAP committed "more than €1 billion over the next four years" to Prior Labs for "the structured data that underpins the world's businesses" ([SAP, July 17](https://news.sap.com/2026/07/sap-completes-prior-labs-acquisition/)).
  - SAP updated its profit outlook "to reflect dilutive impact from Dremio and Prior Labs acquisitions." Its chief executive: customers want "accurate and compliant AI outcomes grounded in their most critical business processes and data" ([SAP, July 23](https://news.sap.com/2026/07/sap-announces-q2-and-half-year-2026-results/)). Dremio is "an open, high-performance data lakehouse platform built to accelerate agentic AI" ([StockTitan copy of SAP statement, July 23](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/SAP/sap-quarterly-statement-q2-mu9r28yruw3r.html)). Observed spend. Reason claimed.
  - Progress paid "$400 million in cash" for a business that "helps customers move faster with trusted data" ([Domo release via Yahoo Finance, July 22](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/domo-announces-agreement-sell-substantially-201500810.html)).
  - Pharma instance: Doceree's Semmelweis model runs on an "identity graph of more than 6 million verified health care providers" and optimizes "toward filled and refilled prescriptions" ([Pharmaceutical Commerce, July 21](https://www.pharmaceuticalcommerce.com/view/inside-semmelweis-docerees-ai-bet-on-pharma-marketing)). No outcome data. Claimed.
- **Evidence strength: MEDIUM. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** Two independent vendors put money behind it. Seller side only.
- **Obvious explanation.** Model makers squeeze software makers, so software makers move to what they own: business data.

**HYPOTHESIS UPDATE**
- **Hypothesis affected.** "Customer data can be technically available while remaining operationally untrustworthy." Primary pillar: Foundation. Also Trust and Control. Territory: Decision Integrity Under Complexity.
- **Direction: STRENGTHENS.** The biggest sellers now price trusted data as what AI depends on. That backs his core claim from the supply side, not yet from buyers.

**PATTERN BASIS**
- SAP and Progress point the same way with money. Doceree shows the same logic in pharma marketing.
- Against it: Domo was sold cheap and stays public to keep more than $900 million in past tax losses. Partly a clean-up.
- Accelerating, seller side only.

**NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS**
- **KEY INSIGHT: When vendors sell trust as a feature, buyers stop checking it.** "Trusted by whom, on what evidence?" moves from buyer to vendor. Someone independent still has to say what green proves.
- **KEY INSIGHT: Doceree makes provider identity the single point of failure.** A model tuned to filled prescriptions is only as good as the match between a provider record and a real prescriber. A bad match teaches it the wrong lesson, with confidence.

**CHALLENGE THE INTERPRETATION**
- **What else could explain this?** Ordinary consolidation.
- **Counterevidence.** No buyer spend, no outcome data, one distressed seller.
- **What would make us wrong?** Buyers buy agents directly, skip data work, and results hold up.

**TRIGGER**
- A pharma commercial or agency buyer names data trust or provider identity as the reason to buy or pause.
- A second pharma marketing vendor ties AI to prescription outcomes and publishes measured results.

**VALUE**
- **Fast value.** Ask any leader shown a "trusted data" pitch: "Trusted by whom, and what check would show it is wrong?"
- **Deeper asset.** Not yet. Needs buyer-side evidence.
- **Learning question.** When a vendor promises trusted data, who inside the pharma company verifies it?
- **Long-term value.** If buyers start asking for independent proof, the diagnostic becomes the check before a purchase, not after a failure.

**DECISION**
- **What changed?** Vendors now pay for the trusted-data idea, not just say it.
- **Recommended state: MONITOR.**
- **Why this state?** Real on the seller side. No buyer behavior yet.
- **What moves it next?** A buyer naming data trust as a reason to buy or stop moves this to TALK.

## Signal 2: Enterprise AI is in paid production. Pharma commercial is silent in public.

**SIGNAL**
- **What happened.** Observed: three technology sellers reported AI revenue and use at scale. Observed: pharma statements this week put AI in research and diagnostics only. Interpretation: pharma commercial AI is small or unreported.
- **Unit of analysis.** Large enterprises in general, compared with how pharma companies describe AI in public.
- **Evidence.**
  - ServiceNow's AI "crossed $1 billion in annual contract value," and "agentic deployments of ServiceNow AI increased ninefold in just nine months" ([ServiceNow, July 22](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)).
  - IBM: "As AI adoption moves from experimentation to enterprise-scale deployment, the company is also investing in more specialized technical and client-facing talent" ([IBM, July 22](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-22-IBM-RELEASES-SECOND-QUARTER-RESULTS)).
  - Alphabet: "Cloud backlog grew to $514 billion" and "We continue to be supply constrained" ([Google, July 22](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/)).
  - Novartis mentions AI only as a risk: "the development or adoption of new technologies, including artificial intelligence" ([Novartis, July 21](https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-delivered-sales-growth-q2-and-further-advanced-pipeline-full-year-guidance-reaffirmed)).
  - Roche's AI lines are in diagnostics, "with advanced AI analysis and workflow capabilities" for its "digital pathology portfolio" ([Roche, July 23](https://www.roche.com/investors/updates/inv-update-2026-07-23)). Bristol Myers Squibb's AI computing expansion is research only ([Bristol Myers Squibb, July 20](https://news.bms.com/news/corporate-financial/2026/Bristol-Myers-Squibb-to-Build-the-Most-Powerful-AI-Factory-in-Life-Sciences-with-NVIDIA/default.aspx)).
  - Cost is unsettled: Anthropic cut limits on its top model again, and lower-tier users "have to pay API prices," meaning pay-per-use through the application programming interface ([The Decoder, July 18](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-slashes-claude-fable-5-limits-in-max-and-team-premium-and-pushes-pro-users-toward-api-pricing/)).
- **Evidence strength: MEDIUM. CONFIRMING EVIDENCE for the general shift. EARLY INDICATOR for the pharma gap.** Three sellers agree. For pharma, an absence is weak proof.
- **Obvious explanation.** Pharma talks about AI where investors reward it: research.

**HYPOTHESIS UPDATE**
- **Hypothesis affected.** "AI deployment advances faster than the organization can define value, redesign the workflow, and prove the result." Primary pillar: Use. Also Operating Model.
- **Direction: COMPLICATES.** Deployment moves in general. In pharma commercial we cannot see it, so we cannot tell if it outruns proof or is absent.

**PATTERN BASIS**
- Seller data is consistent and stronger than Wednesday. Roche did not change the pharma read.
- Seller revenue, not buyer outcomes. Stable.

**NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS**
- **KEY INSIGHT: Pharma commercial AI may be bought through others.** If it runs inside customer relationship management vendors and agencies, pharma never reports it. The accountable leader owns the result without owning the system. That is his accountability condition in plain form.

**CHALLENGE THE INTERPRETATION**
- **What else could explain this?** Commercial AI may come up on earnings calls, which we did not review.
- **Counterevidence.** Vendors like Doceree build AI for pharma commercial work, so someone expects pharma to buy. Inferred, not observed spend.
- **What would make us wrong?** A pharma company reports a commercial AI result with a number.

**TRIGGER**
- A pharma earnings call names a commercial AI outcome, such as field time saved or prescriptions gained, with a figure.
- An agency reports AI revenue from pharma clients.

**VALUE**
- **Fast value.** Ask a commercial leader: "Which of your AI runs inside a vendor's product, and who checks what it decides?"
- **Deeper asset.** Not yet.
- **Learning question.** Do pharma commercial leaders know which AI decisions happen inside their vendors' tools?
- **Long-term value.** If often not, a map of where vendor AI decides becomes the starting point for governance work.

**DECISION**
- **What changed?** The general shift is confirmed. The pharma commercial gap held.
- **Recommended state: INVESTIGATE.**
- **Why this state?** The gap is in his lane, and late-July pharma earnings calls are a cheap place to look.
- **What moves it next?** One pharma commercial AI number moves it to TALK.

## Signal 3: Patients can now link medical records to a general AI

**SIGNAL**
- **What happened.** Observed: on July 23, OpenAI opened ChatGPT Health to all United States adults. Users can connect "medical records from hospital systems like Epic and Oracle Health." It handles "300 million health-related queries weekly, up from 230 million" in January ([TechCrunch, July 23](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/openai-makes-chatgpt-health-available-to-all-u-s-users/)). OpenAI's terms say it is "not intended for use in the diagnosis or treatment of any health condition."
- **Unit of analysis.** United States consumer health behavior.
- **Evidence.** One primary event, company numbers, no independent usage data.
- **Evidence strength: MEDIUM. EARLY INDICATOR.** Large scale. Pharma impact unmeasured.
- **Obvious explanation.** A consumer product reaching its full market.

**HYPOTHESIS UPDATE**
- **Hypothesis affected.** Territory: Context Continuity and High-Context AI, meaning how AI carries context without gaining invisible authority. Primary pillar: Trust and Control.
- **Direction: COMPLICATES.** A system outside pharma's reviewed channels now holds a patient's record and answers treatment questions.

**PATTERN BASIS**
- Interesting signal. No pattern yet. It made the list on scale alone.

**NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS**
- **KEY INSIGHT: The fullest patient record may soon sit in a chatbot, not in pharma's customer view.** The single customer view pharma has chased may form outside pharma, held by a third party.

**CHALLENGE THE INTERPRETATION**
- **What else could explain this?** Use may be general wellness, not drug decisions.
- **Counterevidence.** No data on questions about prescriptions or brands.
- **What would make us wrong?** Few users actually connect records.

**TRIGGER**
- Evidence that patients or providers act on these answers about specific treatments.
- A pharma company changes medical information or patient support because of it.

**VALUE**
- **Fast value.** Ask a patient services leader: "What does ChatGPT tell a patient about your drug, and who checked?"
- **Deeper asset.** Not yet.
- **Learning question.** Do pharma teams watch what general AI tells patients about their therapies?
- **Long-term value.** Too early.

**DECISION**
- **What changed?** A new channel with scale sits between pharma and patients.
- **Recommended state: MONITOR.**
- **Why this state?** Real scale, unmeasured pharma impact.
- **What moves it next?** Evidence of patients acting on treatment answers.
