## Signal 1: Deals expand before anyone shows results

**SIGNAL**
- **What happened.** Observed: an AI deal expanded with no disclosed results, a vendor counted pharma adoption its own way, and four surveys found high use, low measurement. Interpretation: commitments grow faster than anyone defines "better."
- **Unit of analysis.** How AI spending in commercial and customer work is justified. No survey is pharma.
- **Evidence.**
  - Lundbeck will scale Eversana's AI work across "marketing, medical communications, market access and media operations" ([PR Newswire, August 25](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lundbeck-and-eversana-announce-strategic-partnership-to-advance-ai-powered-commercialization-302858538.html)). No metrics. A diligence brief: "No deal value, contract duration or quantified Lundbeck-specific productivity target was disclosed." Google Cloud's speed and cost figures are "platform evidence, not verified Lundbeck-specific performance" ([Lucidquest, August 26](https://www.lqventures.com/lundbeck-eversana-ai-commercialization/)).
  - Salesforce reported agent revenue over $1.5 billion a year ([Salesforce, August 26](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/fy27-q2-earnings/)). As reported from the call, its life sciences product counts "all five of the largest pharmaceutical companies," and the outcome cited was its own contract value ([Yahoo Finance, August 26](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/salesforce-q2-earnings-call-highlights-230418017.html)). Vendor claim.
  - Talkdesk: "only 5% can quantify AI's impact on business outcomes" ([Talkdesk, August 25](https://www.talkdesk.com/news-and-press/press-releases/state-of-agentic-automation-cx-2026/)). Vendor survey.
  - Infosys, 1,000-plus executives: "Two-thirds said their organization struggles to measure the ROI," meaning return on investment ([CIO Dive, August 26](https://www.ciodive.com/news/CIOs-waiting-AI-savings-ROI/828883/)).
  - Protiviti, 902 finance leaders: 77% deployed, "only 35% of them can confidently gauge the return" ([CFO Dive, August 27](https://www.cfodive.com/news/only-35-percent-finance-leaders-confidently-gauge-roi-ai-protiviti-cybersecurity-it/829015/)).
  - WalkMe, 2,000 workers: nine in ten confident, a quarter said AI worked the first try ([CIO Dive, August 27](https://www.ciodive.com/news/employees-overstate-ai-skills-report/828895/)). Vendor survey.
- **Evidence strength: MEDIUM. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** Four sponsors, one shape, one buyer act. No drug maker has published a checked result.
- **Obvious explanation.** Companies keep commercial results private.

**HYPOTHESIS UPDATE**
- **Hypothesis affected.** "AI deployment advances faster than the organization can define value, redesign the workflow, and prove the result." Territory: Decision Integrity Under Complexity. Primary pillar: Trust and Control.
- **Direction: STRENGTHENS.** Last week the gap was opinion. This week money crossed it.

**PATTERN BASIS**
- Fifth week running. Buyer act, earnings, surveys, and a diligence brief agree.
- Against: an expansion hints private results were good. The Lundbeck coverage is one event, counted once.
- Accelerating, now in behavior.

**NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS**
- **KEY INSIGHT: When the buyer does not define the measure, the seller does.** Salesforce counts contract value. Google Cloud reports platform speed. Veeva calls its field output "Commercial Evidence" (Signal 2). Inferred: the gap is being filled, just not by the buyer.
- **KEY INSIGHT: The gap is definition, not pressure.** Infosys found "the rush to prove short-term ROI is inhibiting their ability to experiment." Inferred: pressure is high. An agreed "better," set up front, is missing.

**CHALLENGE THE INTERPRETATION**
- **What else could explain this?** Early wins are speed and cost, real but awkward to publish.
- **Counterevidence.** The expansion itself.
- **What would make us wrong?** A drug maker states the baseline behind an expansion, checked by someone other than the vendor.

**TRIGGER**
- Lundbeck, Regeneron, or Biogen names a before-and-after number.
- A pharma survey finds "deployed but can't measure" in commercial AI.

**VALUE**
- **Fast value.** Ask: "Before you expanded, what number did you look at, and who produced it?"
- **Deeper asset.** Last week's baseline check, plus: who produced the number.
- **Learning question.** Does the evidence behind an expansion come from the vendor or the buyer's own team?
- **Long-term value.** A record of where vendor numbers and buyer results split becomes the asset.

**DECISION**
- **What changed?** The proof gap now shows in buying.
- **Recommended state: TALK.**
- **Why this state?** Cheap to ask, with a named case.
- **What moves it next?** Two buyers who say the vendor supplied the evidence. Then TEST.

## Signal 2: Big drug makers rebuild the customer record with AI inside

**SIGNAL**
- **What happened.** Observed: two large drug makers committed to a new customer relationship management system on August 25. Interpretation: the customer record at many large drug makers is being rebuilt now, with AI in it from day one.
- **Unit of analysis.** Customer system choices at the largest drug makers, and the sales call record.
- **Evidence.**
  - Regeneron will "generate Commercial Evidence from AI-powered field engagement." The AI call report captures insights "previously lost to dropdowns and compliance concerns" ([PR Newswire, August 25](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/veeva-vault-crm-selected-by-regeneron-302858885.html)). Biogen committed the same day ([PR Newswire, August 25](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/veeva-vault-crm-selected-by-biogen-302858894.html)). Observed.
  - Veeva: 12 of the top 20 committed, one top-20 drug maker running the AI call report across its full United States field team ([PR Newswire, August 26](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/veeva-announces-fiscal-2027-second-quarter-results-302860933.html)). Vendor claim.
  - On customers who chose Salesforce: "those implementations have encountered delays and challenges because of the complexity of the product" ([Yahoo Finance, August 26](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/veeva-systems-q2-earnings-call-230419426.html)). Win-backs expected "in 2027 and 2028" ([Zacks via Yahoo Finance, August 27](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/veev-q2-earnings-call-centers-143700367.html)). A rival's claim.
- **Evidence strength: MEDIUM. EARLY INDICATOR.** Two buyer commitments are observed. Rollout and delay claims are vendors'.
- **Obvious explanation.** A forced platform choice. Buyers pick a side.

**HYPOTHESIS UPDATE**
- **Hypothesis affected.** "Teams need shared definitions, ownership, provenance, and repeatability before more automation raises the cost of inconsistency." Territory: Continuity of Meaning. Primary pillar: Foundation. Also Meaning and Context.
- **Direction: STRENGTHENS.** His trigger moment, AI added before definitions are ready, is running on a schedule at the largest buyers.

**PATTERN BASIS**
- Buyer acts, earnings, and a competitor statement agree.
- Against: system moves are routine. No buyer named a definition problem.
- Emerging.

**NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS**
- **KEY INSIGHT: The dropdown was a definition.** It made a rep pick from agreed answers. The AI call report swaps that for a model's summary. Inferred: more comes in, less of it has agreed meaning. Who checks the summary before it counts as "evidence"?
- **KEY INSIGHT: A system move is where mismatched numbers start.** With win-backs expected, some drug makers may move twice. Inferred: each move quietly changes local definitions.

**CHALLENGE THE INTERPRETATION**
- **What else could explain this?** Earnings-week talk.
- **Counterevidence.** Regeneron frames the move as speed. No buyer mentions data trouble.
- **What would make us wrong?** Reports match before and after, and AI summaries are checked against a fixed field list.

**TRIGGER**
- A drug maker or agency says in public that reports did not match after a system move.
- A job posting to validate AI-written call notes.

**VALUE**
- **Fast value.** Ask: "When the dropdown goes away, what replaces the agreed list, and who checks the summary?"
- **Deeper asset.** A short check before a system move: which fields carry definitions, which reports depend on them. Only after talks confirm the pain.
- **Learning question.** In a system move, does anyone own keeping definitions the same on both sides?
- **Long-term value.** A before-and-after check of the customer record could become a repeatable way in.

**DECISION**
- **What changed?** Many large drug makers are rebuilding the customer record, with AI in it.
- **Recommended state: INVESTIGATE.**
- **Why this state?** Buyer behavior at scale in his domain. The meaning risk is my inference.
- **What moves it next?** One data leader confirms AI-written notes feed reports unchecked. Then TALK.

## Signal 3: A drug maker buys the operating model, still only one

**SIGNAL**
- **What happened.** Observed: Lundbeck bought "an AI-powered operating model" from a services firm ([Fierce Pharma, August 25](https://www.fiercepharma.com/marketing/lundbeck-strikes-expanded-pact-eversana-broaden-ai-scope-across-us-commercial-org)). No second drug maker was named by Friday.
- **Unit of analysis.** Whether drug makers build or buy the redesign of commercial work around AI.
- **Evidence.**
  - Fierce Pharma calls Lundbeck Eversana's "first publicly named pharma partner."
  - Deloitte, 501 United States leaders: "Only 1 in 5" are "prepared to redesign business processes to run autonomously with AI agents," held back by "inconsistent and fragmented data and systems" ([CIO Dive, August 21](https://www.ciodive.com/news/only-1-in-5-organizations-are-prepared-to-move-toward-autonomous-ai-agents/828445/)). Not pharma.
- **Evidence strength: LOW. EARLY INDICATOR.** One buyer.
- **Obvious explanation.** A mid-size drug maker outsourcing work it cannot staff.

**HYPOTHESIS UPDATE**
- **Hypothesis affected.** "The accountable leader receives the uncertainty that upstream teams and systems did not resolve." Primary pillar: Operating Model.
- **Direction: STRENGTHENS.** Last week only sellers named it. Now a buyer paid. It complicates the opportunity: the buyer chose a firm that runs the work, not a check of what it proves.

**PATTERN BASIS**
- Two services firms, Axtria and Eversana, sell embedded AI work to pharma. One buyer. Stable since Tuesday.

**NON-OBVIOUS ANALYSIS**
- **KEY INSIGHT: When the firm that runs the work also reports on it, no one checks it.** Inferred: a neutral check fits beside that deal, not instead of it.

**CHALLENGE THE INTERPRETATION**
- **What else could explain this?** "Operating model" is marketing for managed services.
- **Counterevidence.** No structure change at Lundbeck on record.
- **What would make us wrong?** The deal proves to be a content tool.

**TRIGGER**
- A second drug maker named as an Eversana or Axtria client.

**VALUE**
- **Fast value.** Ask: "If the partner runs the work and reports the results, who on your side checks them?"
- **Deeper asset.** Not yet.
- **Learning question.** Do buyers of these deals keep anyone inside who owns the result?
- **Long-term value.** If rarely, a check beside run-the-work partners becomes an advisory role.

**DECISION**
- **What changed?** The fix sellers described is now bought, once.
- **Recommended state: MONITOR.**
- **Why this state?** One buyer.
- **What moves it next?** A second named buyer. Then INVESTIGATE.
