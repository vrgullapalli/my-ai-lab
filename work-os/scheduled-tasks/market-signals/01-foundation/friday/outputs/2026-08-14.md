## Signal 2: Sellers of pharma data describe careful buyers, and argue for quality

**What happened**
- Observed: two data sellers reported falling revenue.
- Claimed: one says customers who chose cheaper data came back.

**Unit of analysis:** data vendors selling to pharma commercial teams, describing their own buyers.

**Evidence**
- OptimizeRx, August 12: "Revenue decreased 30% to $20.5 million." Average revenue per top 20 drug maker fell from $3,095 thousand to $2,659 thousand. Their share of revenue fell from 59% to 54% ([OptimizeRx release via GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/12/3344034/0/en/optimizerx-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-and-updates-fiscal-year-2026-guidance.html)). Observed.
- On the call, management tied the drop to "a limited number of large customers" and "MFN pricing dynamics," meaning most favored nation drug pricing ([OptimizeRx call via Investing.com, August 12](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-optimizerx-beats-q2-2026-profit-view-as-shares-slip-93CH-4856270)). Claimed.
- Definitive Healthcare, August 10: revenue was "a decrease of 9%" ([Definitive release via GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/10/3342208/0/en/definitive-healthcare-reports-financial-results-for-second-quarter-2026.html)). Chief executive Kevin Coop: "the cost of an inferior data set outweighs the savings," and "The response in our life sciences segment has been slower" ([Definitive call via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-definitive-healthcare-tops-q2-2026-eps-forecast-93CH-4850490)). Claimed.
- Counterevidence: OptimizeRx's AI audience platform grew "over 30% year-over-year."

**Evidence strength: LOW. REINFORCING INDICATOR.** Real revenue numbers. The quality claim is a seller explaining itself.

**Obvious explanation:** drug pricing pressure squeezes marketing budgets, and data vendors feel it first.

**Hypothesis update**
- Affected: "Customer data can be technically available while remaining operationally untrustworthy," and the diagnostic-to-advisory opportunity. Primary: Foundation (data quality, spend). Secondary: Use.
- Direction: **COMPLICATES.** The quality line helps the belief a little. Careful budgets make a paid diagnostic harder to start.

**Pattern basis:** a third seller in two weeks, after Doximity's "less than 10% of overall budgets." Repeating, now with revenue numbers. Still no buyer voice, and OptimizeRx's drop may be a few accounts, not a market.

## Non-obvious analysis

**KEY INSIGHT: "Which vendor" is being settled before "which data"**
- Easy to miss: Novo's two releases name models, clouds, agents, and functions. Neither names a customer data source, a definition, or a measure.
- Why it matters: when the vendor comes first, the data work takes the vendor's shape. The leader later asked "what can we trust" may find the answer sits with two outside companies. Inferred.

**KEY INSIGHT: The only voice pricing bad data is the one selling data**
- Easy to miss: no buyer measured what cheaper data cost them. The seller says so, and benefits.
- Why it matters: buyers who cannot measure weak data switch on price and come back on pain. That is the need "a fast, bounded way to see what their current foundation can actually support." Inferred.

## Challenge the interpretation

- **What else could explain this?** Novo is a research story, and the marketing line is Amazon selling ads and pharmacy services. The seller drops are drug pricing politics. Both could be normal business, not a Foundation shift.
- **Counterevidence:** 25,000 Novo users suggests a platform that works at scale for simple jobs. Definitive's strong biopharma quarter. Pfizer's line last week.
- **What would make us wrong:** Novo keeps customer data in its own systems. A buyer cuts data vendors with no loss. Amgen names one stolen login.

## Trigger

- Novo or Amazon names customer, field, or prescriber data in the deal, or a result measure.
- A second drug company names one "preferred" cloud and AI vendor with commercial scope.
- A drug company, not a seller, says it cut or re-bought data vendors.
- Amgen names the entry method, or a second drug company files a vendor-hosted breach.

## Value

- **FAST VALUE:** ask a leader: "For each AI or data vendor you picked this year, name the customer data it reads, who defines it, and who checks it." Blank answers mean the vendor ran ahead of the foundation.
- **DEEPER ASSET:** not yet. One company, two weeks. It could later join last week's "what you rely on but do not run" section.
- **LEARNING QUESTION:** When a commercial team switches data vendors to save money, does anyone measure what the switch cost in rework, mismatched numbers, or lost trust? Who?
- **LONG-TERM VALUE:** a short "cost of weaker data" check, then buyer answers, then a vendor part of the diagnostic, then a before-and-after measure that travels.
