YOUR JOB TODAY: the Pharma Intelligence Watch

Search current public sources for meaningful changes in AI-enabled brand, marketing, competitive, commercial, scientific, and regulatory intelligence for pharma.

THE BASELINE (from Venkat's own market study, dated 2026-08-30)

The market has strong tools for individual intelligence tasks but remains weak at carrying evidence and company context through a complete decision, action, and learning loop.

What the study found:
- Three markets that get talked about as one. Competitive intelligence is mature and dominated by pharma-native data incumbents (Clarivate's Cortellis, now sold to Altaris; Norstella's Citeline and Evaluate; IQVIA; GlobalData), with AlphaSense as a horizontal search layer across the top. Marketing and omnichannel intelligence is mature and owned by Veeva, IQVIA, ZS, Axtria, Indegene, and a media-side group (Doceree, DeepIntent, OptimizeRx, Real Chemistry, PurpleLab). Brand intelligence (brand health, perception, message resonance, share of voice) is the least served and least integrated. No vendor owns the whole picture, and no buyer does either.
- The data layer is in revenue decline while an intelligence layer is being built on top of it. Verified in filings: Clarivate sold its life sciences segment for 600 million dollars with segment revenue down 6.9 percent; OptimizeRx revenue down 30 percent year over year; Aktana acquired after a 43 percent headcount drop; Komodo Health layoffs.
- A new agentic deliverable layer launched in 2025 and 2026: Norstella Atlas CI, ZoomRx Ferma, Sleuth, Metly, Veeva AI Agents, IQVIA.ai, Trinity InsightsEDGE Brand Hub, Real Chemistry Anatomi, ZS ZAIDYN, ODAIA, Tellius, Causaly.
- Of roughly 50 credible offerings, 6 to 8 are genuinely AI-native and 10 to 12 are credibly agentic. Almost all of that is agentic assembly, not agentic judgment: the systems fetch, reconcile, synthesize, and format, but do not take a position. Two incumbents admitted this against their own interest. Clarivate: AI does not materially change insight generation, stakeholder communication, or scenario modeling. Evaluate: 'little is left to interpret the insight or act on it.'
- Buyers: competitive intelligence teams of 3 to 5 people with external budgets mostly between 100 and 500 thousand dollars, whose top objective is early threat detection.
- The important gaps, ranked: (1) interpretation and recommendation, the market stops at 'what changed' and never reaches 'what it means for you and what to do'; (2) signal-to-action latency, a 9 to 12 week competitive response window; (3) institutional memory, no vendor keeps a living model of the company's own strategy and prior decisions; (4) no measurement of how a brand is represented inside AI answers to clinical questions; (5) traceability to primary source is now the dominant failure mode of AI-assisted intelligence; (6) nobody closes the loop between a signal raised and what actually happened. Also: brand is the only major pharma function without an owner or a system of work; competitor creative is an unread record of regulatory decisions; joining external signal to internal data is NOT whitespace (disconfirmed); mid-cap and pre-commercial access is closing and becoming a pricing fight.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR
- New vendors, products, and major features. AI-native or agentic launches.
- Acquisitions, funding, and partnerships.
- Named pharma buyers or customers, and credible outcome evidence.
- Evidence that the gaps in decision context, cross-domain synthesis, correctability, disagreement, and learning from outcomes are being closed, or are widening.
- Regulatory or scientific intelligence moves that change what a brand or commercial team can do.

Filter out marketing noise, minor feature releases, repeated coverage of the same event, and unsupported claims. Treat vendor case studies and announcements as evidence of vendor behavior, not proof of customer outcomes. Watch for the parent-versus-product credibility trick, where a consultancy's client list is quietly attributed to a new product.

THE THRESHOLD
Notify only when something materially changes the market map, vendor capabilities, buyer adoption, the competitive landscape, or the identified gaps. If nothing material has changed since the last run, do not produce a brief.

1. Find today's date in America/Chicago time. Write dates as YYYY-MM-DD.
2. Memory first. Call the Artifact tool with action 'list' and limit 50. If an artifact titled 'Pharma Intelligence Watch: Run Log' exists, read it with action 'read' to learn the date of the last run and what was already reported. Also read the two most recent artifacts whose title begins with 'Pharma Intelligence Watch' and carries a date.
3. Research what changed since the last run date, or the last 14 days on the first run.
4. If something material changed: write the brief to /home/user/brief.html as a simple, clean web page (a <title> of exactly 'Pharma Intelligence Watch YYYY-MM-DD', one small <style> block, headings, paragraphs, bullets, and links; no scripts, no external files). For each meaningful change, explain: what happened; how strong the evidence is (observed, vendor claim, inferred, or unknown); why it matters against the baseline; what belief should change; what to watch next. Link to primary sources. Publish it with the Artifact tool (file_path /home/user/brief.html, favicon '🔬', description one sentence). Then send exactly one mobile push notification with the PushNotification tool: the headline, two lines, and the artifact URL. Then print the complete brief as your final message.
5. Whether or not anything changed, update the Run Log. Write /home/user/run-log.html with a <title> of exactly 'Pharma Intelligence Watch: Run Log' and the full log as a bullet list, newest first, with one new line for today: the date, the sources checked, and either the title of the brief you published or 'no material change'. If a Run Log artifact already exists, publish with the Artifact tool passing its URL as 'url' so it updates in place; otherwise publish it new with favicon '🗂️'.
6. If nothing material changed, do not send a push. Your final message is three lines at most: 'No material change since YYYY-MM-DD', what you checked, and the one or two items that came closest and why they did not clear the bar.
7. If the Artifact tool fails, still print the result as your final message and say at the top that publishing failed. Do not use Google Drive for anything.
