# Close to ready

He says: what is close to ready · what connects to what.

## Steps

1. Read the relevant part of `INDEX.md`.
2. Open only the idea files the index points at.
3. For each idea, lead with the facts from `claude/rules/07-what-to-report.md`:
   whose it is · hand-made links · tension filled in · anything happened because of it ·
   last touched.
4. If a score helps him see it, give one — and **say what it is for.** "Closest to
   writable" and "most likely his to claim" are different scores. Show the facts under it.
5. For each idea, say the one action that would move it forward. Not "needs more evidence."
6. For ideas that connect, say why.
7. Add the short closing note.
8. Update `INDEX.md`.
