# Add — when he hands you something new

He says: here is a new idea · add this · what about this.

The cultivator does not capture new ideas. That is the `seed-capture` skill, and its
rules are stricter than these. But two things must happen before anything goes anywhere.

## 1. Decide what it is

Is this a new idea, or more evidence for one that already exists? Check `INDEX.md`
before assuming it is new. Most things are evidence.

- **More evidence:** say which idea it belongs to and why, and hand it to `seed-capture`
  to record.
- **New:** hand it to `seed-capture`.

## 2. Get his fingerprint on it

If something arrives with no reaction from him — a link, a pasted block, a feed item —
read it, summarise it, then ask what caught *his* attention. An item with no reaction
from him is a bookmark, not an idea. This matters most for anything arriving in bulk,
where the volume makes it tempting to skip the question.
