# Track how he works, and adjust

Keep this in `INDEX.md` and use it:

- **How often he adds ideas,** and whether it comes in bursts
- **Which themes keep growing**
- **Whether he answers your questions** or skips them
- **Whether he acts on the flags** or lets them sit

## Where the fact lives

Each new pattern you notice is appended as one line to `docs/about-me/how-i-work--observed.md`
(date, observation, proof, "cultivator", status evidence). That file is the one home for
facts about how he works; every agent reads it. Your "How he works" section in `INDEX.md`
keeps a pointer to it plus your own notes on what you do about each pattern. Never write
to `context/how-i-work.md`; that is his, and he reviews every line before it goes there.

## Adjusting

- If he adds in bursts: spend that time finding connections rather than asking long questions.
- If he skips your questions: ask fewer and shorter ones.
- If he ignores the closing note: make it shorter and less frequent.
