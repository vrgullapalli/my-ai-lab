# Executive demand experience gate — conditional commercial overlay

Apply ONLY when the asset also carries a commercial role: discovery,
warming, proof, lead generation, or conversion. The commercial role is
an overlay; the asset is never redefined as "a lead-gen asset" and its
primary user job stays primary. This is a reference standard — never a
separate skill.

Governing principle: **lead generation must be a consequence of value,
not a substitute for it.**

Evaluate:

- **Executive relevance** — feels relevant to their situation, role,
  company, and decisions without pretending to know more than we do.
- **Time to value** — something useful happens before any meaningful
  commitment is requested.
- **Insight over novelty** — the reaction to design for is "that's
  useful," never "that's clever AI."
- **Credibility** — personalization and claims accurate enough to
  increase trust; wrong personalization is worse than light
  personalization.
- **Progressive personalization** — never ten questions upfront; infer
  only what can be inferred safely; ask only what materially improves
  the result.
- **Non-creepy personalization** — the read-aloud test, again: would a
  reasonable executive wonder "why does this stranger know that about
  me?" If yes, reduce or remove. Person-level research stays out of
  the surface.
- **Value before identification** — before any ask for email, company
  details, identity, or calendar time: has enough value already been
  delivered that the request is proportionate? (Standing ruling:
  never email-gate the proof.)
- **Conversion continuity** — the next commercial step follows from
  the value delivered. Not "you have inconsistent definitions — book a
  call," but "if this affects a decision you are making now, the next
  useful step is determining which definition that decision actually
  requires" — with a human conversation as one option.
- **Progressive proof** — the settled rule applies: depth of proof
  matches depth of permission.
- **No bait-and-switch** — never promise an assessment and deliver
  three vague sentences behind a sales gate; useful value is never
  withheld solely to force conversion.
- **Shareability** — the asset stays useful when forwarded internally;
  commercial success never depends on the original visitor staying in
  the flow.

## Quality dimensions — never averaged

Judgment quality (is the reasoning good?), executive experience
quality (can they understand and use it?), and commercial experience
quality (does value lead naturally toward a relationship without
damaging trust?) are assessed separately. One score never hides
another's failure; a near-zero on any important dimension is grounds
to revise or refuse.
