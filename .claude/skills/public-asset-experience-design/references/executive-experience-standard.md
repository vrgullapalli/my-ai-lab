# Executive experience standard — conditional

Apply when the intended person is a senior executive or leader, AND/OR
the asset performs consequential interpretation, recommendation,
evaluation, or decision support. This is a reference standard the
experience-design and QA skills apply — never a separate skill.

Governing principle: **the unit of quality is not the artifact the AI
generated; it is the quality of the executive's resulting
understanding, judgment, and ability to act.** And its corollary:
**show less when less produces better judgment.**

> Standing context, Venkat 2026-08-30: he is "not a big fan of the
> current executive instruments" in the existing corpus — and notes
> they were built with earlier-generation models (Opus 4 era). Those
> instruments are prior art to learn from, never the benchmark, and
> their ceiling is not the ceiling. Design from this standard, not
> from resemblance to existing instruments; when this standard and an
> existing instrument's pattern disagree, the standard wins.

The design must satisfy:

- **Orientation** — within seconds, what matters here?
- **Significance** — why does it deserve attention at all?
- **Change** — when recurring or stateful: what is new, changed,
  improved, deteriorated, corrected, stale, or superseded — without
  rereading everything. "Three things changed; one changes the
  recommendation" beats re-showing 34 facts.
- **Judgment** — the asset states what it thinks or what needs
  attention; it never merely displays information.
- **Trust** — important claims trace to current evidence when
  challenged, in the order conclusion → reasoning → evidence; the
  primary experience is never buried in citations.
- **Uncertainty** — inference, ambiguity, disagreement, missing
  evidence, and confidence limits are represented honestly.
- **Falsifiability** — the output can say what would change this
  reading.
- **Scope** — what this helps decide, and what it explicitly does NOT
  decide, are both clear.
- **Correctability** — the executive can disagree, clarify, correct,
  or supply missing context without breaking the experience.
- **History and truth states** — where relevant, current, previous,
  corrected, superseded, and stale are DISTINCT states. Historical
  context may inform judgment; it never masquerades as current
  evidence, and history is not silently deleted when it explains why a
  judgment changed.
- **Semantic consistency** — for generative assets, stabilize meaning,
  not wording: materially equivalent evidence produces materially
  equivalent truth state, prioritization, interpretation, decision
  relevance, and recommended action, even when prose varies. Exact
  text matching is required only when the asset promises determinism.
- **Action** — what now needs a decision, a question, an
  investigation, an owner, a pause, an escalation — or nothing.
- **Attention** — protected deliberately; the system never repeats
  everything it knows.
- **Portability** — another senior person, receiving the result cold,
  understands the situation, what matters, why, the evidence and its
  limits, what changed, and what happens next.
