# Wording lenses, for step 5 of seed-capture

> Added 2026-09-12 at Venkat's word (01:54: "also scan for any wordings that can be created. apply a dave chappelle and seth godin lenses"; 02:04: "yes" to how it runs). Sample run that earned it: four sessions, in receipt R-2026-09-12-0150-b4b8's session. One page. No new skill, no new mode.

## What a wording is

A short sentence or phrase that could become part of his working vocabulary: a rule line, a seed title, a reply line, or a line in a post. Sentences, never names or labels for systems (the front door bans invented labels).

Two parts, asked in this order:

1. **Fixed.** Wording that got settled in the session: a term, a rule sentence, a distinction, or a correction. The four classes above in step 5. Verbatim or near-verbatim only.
2. **Could be created.** Wording the session's material makes possible but nobody said flat. Found with the two lenses below.

## The two lenses

**Chappelle.** The true thing everyone in the room half-knows and nobody said out loud. Plain, everyday words. Describe the absurd situation exactly and let the description be the punchline. The edge goes at a situation, a misleading phrase, a contradiction, a number that cannot be true, or a system behaving badly. Never at a person, never at the reader, no named people. Canon section 8 governs: humor is tier 2, in a named room, off by default for executive, website, and first-contact writing.

Example, from the hand-test session: "Eighty-nine signals got a rating. Not one of them ever got a move."

**Godin.** One idea per line. Short and declarative. A reframe that changes what you would do on Monday. Generous, not clever. Often "X is not Y" or "the job is not the output." Ends where the reader can act.

Example, from the proactivity session: "Only resolve what blocks the next layer." (his verbatim)

## How to run it

- Read the session's own record, not memory: the transcript in `evidence/sessions/claude/`, with anchors.
- At most three candidates per lens. More than that means the bar dropped.
- Every candidate carries: the wording · whose it is · anchor · what it is good for (rule line, seed title, reply line, post line).
- **Whose it is**, one of: `his` (he typed or said it) · `adapted` (his idea, tightened) · `new` (the system made it from the material).
- Prefer his and adapted. A new line is allowed and must say so.

## The guard

Wording the system introduced is kept only when Venkat adopts it (his ruling, 2026-08-19). So:

- Scan writes nothing. It shows the list and stops.
- On his pick, a `his` or `adapted` line goes to `memory/concepts.md` with its class, anchor, and scope; a `new` line he picks goes there marked `endorsed`, with whose words they were.
- A line he picks as a seed title goes through Capture, steps 3 to 6, like any seed.
- Zero candidates is a normal result. Never make one to fill the step.

## Output shape, in the close message

```
Wording. Fixed: <n> (listed). Could be created, say the numbers to keep:
1. "<line>" (his | adapted | new) · <lens> · <good for>
2. ...
```
