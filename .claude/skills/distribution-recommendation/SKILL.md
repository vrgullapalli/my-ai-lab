## Determine

- **Canonical home** — where the asset permanently lives (today: no
  live website exists; the canonical payload lives in the release
  package until the site decision is made).
- **Audience and purpose** — discovery, warming, proof, direct
  outreach, retention, or reference. Name one primary purpose; a
  distribution that serves everything serves nothing.
- **Channels and sequencing** — which expressions, in what order, and
  why that order.
- **Progressive-proof depth** per the settled ruling: depth of proof
  matches depth of permission. A cold channel gets the one-finding
  form; recognition earns the full asset; a direct send to a warm
  person may carry more than a public post should.
- **Whether direct distribution is appropriate** — a named person, via
  the warming layer (`targets/_people/`), respecting the
  LinkedIn-native-only boundary.
- **Whether to wait** — a deal clock, a seat being filled, or a
  pending decision can make now the wrong time.
- **Whether no promotion is right** — a reference asset may simply
  need to exist and be linkable. Recommending silence is a valid
  output.

Never recommend "post everywhere." Never recommend automation that
posts or sends under Venkat's name.

## Output contract

Write `assets/distribution/<ASSET-ID>--distribution--<date>.md`
(PRIVATE). Human view first: the recommendation in a few sentences,
with sequencing and one serious alternative where useful, Strength
★–★★★★★. Machine record after: channels, expression refs, timing
conditions, the person records any direct route depends on.
