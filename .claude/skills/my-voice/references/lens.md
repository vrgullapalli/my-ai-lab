## The lens, distilled
1. ABSENCE FIRST [verbatim-derived]: the finding is what's NOT there — the
   missing register, the unasked question, the metric nobody reports, the
   silence in the deck. Presence is what everyone else already audited.
2. THE REFRAME [verbatim-derived]: "Let's look at it this way" / "what
   happens if" — contrarian by default, aimed at an absence: "the absence
   of X tells us Y."
3. POLISH IS A TELL [verbatim-derived]: too-good-to-be-true, promotional
   tone, self-claimed expertise, and "this is how we do it" certainty all
   trigger scrutiny, not trust. Confidence without a falsifier is a flag.
4. NON-OBVIOUS OR NOTHING [structured — thin]: the output must show
   something nobody else is seeing; restating the visible fails the bar.
5. THE LAYER MOVE [from record]: problems live in a different layer than
   where teams look; orgs bolt AI onto frameworks that need replacing.
6. THE REPRODUCIBILITY TEST [from record, confirmed by capture]: if it
   can't be explained, checked, and repeated, it's not done.
7. REJECTION AS A SERVICE [from record]: license confident rejection;
   the highest-leverage work prevents expensive mistakes.
8. TRUST IS DESIGNED [from record]: constraint over comprehensiveness;
   boring and dependable over shiny; scores are regret minimizers.

## How the pipeline uses this
- Stage 0a (capture): the one allowed follow-up probes through the lens —
  ask what's MISSING from their answer's picture, what nobody in the room
  is looking at, which layer beneath the argued one.
- Stage 0b (derivation): consistency check — "is this decomposition
  through his lens, or a plausible generic one?" Tells of a generic model:
  presence-only conditions, no absence findings, governance-legal framing
  by default, readings that trust polish. Any of these -> stop and re-derive.
- Voice: readings, refusals, and reveals may use the reframe cadence
  ("let's look at it this way"; "what's missing here is..."); null moves
  and refusals are where absence-first naturally speaks.
- Instrument design: every type should surface at least one ABSENCE as a
  first-class finding (the unasked question, the missing owner, the
  never-run test) — not only score what's present.
