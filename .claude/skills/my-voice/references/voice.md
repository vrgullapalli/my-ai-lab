AI writes connective tissue only; the voice pass cuts toward his cadence.

## THE DOMINANT FINDING: the voice is INTERROGATIVE
20 of 27 specimens are questions. He argues by asking, then asking one layer
down ("And what do they do?" -> "What's the department called?"). Published
prose sounds AI-written because it is declarative; his voice is not.
Production rule: a piece in his voice OPENS with a question or a "let me"
reframe, carries ONE dialogue-story ("he said / I said"), has ONE concrete
number, and ENDS on a question — not a takeaway.

## Observable markers (from specimens — extend only from new specimens)
- The "let me" openers: "let me back up", "let me put it this way", "let me
  just ask" — the reframe lens with a voice on it
- Stories told as reported dialogue; the argument is the replayed exchange
- Two-beat pressure: restatement, then a shorter incredulous follow-up;
  fragments shrinking ("That's it? You don't do anything?")
- Reactions left in ("Wow, okay." "I hate it.") — AI edits these first
- The wit tag: "See what I did there?" — lands a joke by pointing at it; the
  playful register sits UNDER the blunt one and surfaces in asides
- Short declaratives; fragments as punches ("End-to-end. And have it hold.")
- Escalating number runs as rhythm ("twice, five times, twenty times")
- Threshold idioms ("total stakes", "out of your door")
- The point-blank challenge question, second person
- Self-repair left in; spoken texture kept
- Plain verbs; strong words used plainly ("hate", "break")
- Concrete nouns over categories (five websites, tagged differently — not
  "inconsistent implementations")

## Ban list (AI tells — absence-first applied to prose)
- "isn't just X — it's Y" and all balanced-contrast constructions
- triads of abstract nouns; parallel bullet rhetoric in prose
- "here's the thing", "the reality is", "at the end of the day"
- delve, leverage, robust, seamless, holistic, journey
- uniform sentence lengths; every sentence complete
- hedges (arguably, in many ways); em-dash chains; colon-subtitle headlines

## The voice pass (Stage 2, before the gate; applies to instrument
## readings AND articles)
1. Skeleton first: place his verbatim lines where the weight is.
2. Draft connective tissue only.
3. Read aloud; cut 20-30% — his voice is subtractive, AI's is additive.
4. Run the ban list.
5. Occasional blind test: 3 his + 3 drafted, shuffled. If he can't tell
   instantly, the drafted ones ship; if he can, they don't.
Failure mode this file exists to stop: topic in, generated prose out,
approval at the end. Approval cannot restore what drafting never captured.

## Light clean — the ONLY edits allowed on a specimen
REMOVE: fillers (um, uh, erm, hmm, like-as-filler, you know, I mean, sort
of, kind of, basically, actually-as-filler), stutters and repeated words,
false starts of under three words.
KEEP: self-repairs that carry meaning ("not expert, but..."), fragments,
sentence-final "right?", reactions, odd word order, everything else.
NEVER: complete a sentence, swap a word, merge two sentences, fix grammar,
reorder. If a line is unusable without those, it is not a specimen.
He does not want raw captured; he does not want polish either. This is
the middle, and the verifier matches with fillers ignored on both sides.
