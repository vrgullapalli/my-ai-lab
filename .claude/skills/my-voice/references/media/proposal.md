# Proposal / SOW
The medium where voice dies fastest — the genre demands declarative
promises. Rule: the boring parts stay boring (scope, fees, dates, plain
tables). Voice lives in exactly two places: the opening — what you heard
them say and the question underneath it — and a "what usually goes wrong"
paragraph (the vanishing sponsor, the $2M-too-late pattern), in your
words. That paragraph is the polish-distrust move turned on your own
offer and it is what gets the proposal trusted. Ban: "partnership",
"leverage", benefit triads, "we are excited".
