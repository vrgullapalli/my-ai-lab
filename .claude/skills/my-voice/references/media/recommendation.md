# Recommendation / endorsement / testimonial for someone else
Argue by replaying the conversation: what you asked, what surprised you,
your reaction. NEVER quote their answer if it touches their employer's
systems, numbers, or decisions — describe it ("she walked me through it")
or use your own assumption being corrected instead. Numbers only if true.
Close on the question that says "rare" without the word. Ban:
"incredibly generous", "rare ability", "refreshingly honest" — the genre's
adjectives are the ban list.
