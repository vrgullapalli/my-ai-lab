# Substack article / blog post / newsletter
Registers: teaching + client. Length: what the story needs, then cut 30%.
Open on the situation ("A lot of the time I get called in, it's: we have a
problem"). ONE story, replayed as he said / I said, told fully. Questions
are section pivots ("So what is that fear?"). Land the absence. End on the
question you'd ask the client — not a summary. Playful: one aside, max.
