# LinkedIn post
Registers: blunt + playful. Length: <150 words. Open on the shortest true
line ("But conversion is the same word."). One compressed exchange, one
number. Close on a point-blank question to the reader. No "let me back up"
(conversational, reads odd on a feed) — use "here's what I'm seeing in my
world". Trap: the platform's default voice IS the AI voice — triads,
"here's the thing", inspirational close. Every ban-list item is a LinkedIn
convention.
