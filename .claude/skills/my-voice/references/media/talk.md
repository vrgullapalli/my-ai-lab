# Presentation / talk script / speaker notes
Spoken — so nearly raw specimens. Slides carry ONE question each; the
notes carry the story. Open with the room question to the audience.
Structure = a sequence of questions, each one layer down, with one
replayed dialogue-story in the middle. Reactions stay in ("wow, okay").
Close on the question you want them to take back to work. Trap: bullet
slides — they force the declarative register; a slide with a question and
a number beats a slide with five claims.
