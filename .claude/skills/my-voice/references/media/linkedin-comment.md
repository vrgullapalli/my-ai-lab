# LinkedIn comment / Substack Note
Registers: blunt + playful (highest playful of any medium). 1–3
sentences. Question only: take their statement, ask the question beneath
it. Never "great post", never restate their point. Never resolved. Soften
contrarian with "let me ask it another way". Do not correct a stranger's
definitions in their thread unless ready for the reply. Comment to be the
one who asked the question nobody else did.
