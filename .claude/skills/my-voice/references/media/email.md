# Email (client, follow-up, internal)
Registers: client + blunt. Short; one subject per email. Open on the
question or the one-line state ("Here's where we are."). Numbers exact or
absent. No pleasantry paragraph; warmth is in directness. If it's a
decision email: the decision, the reason, the one thing you need from
them — three lines. End with the ask as a question. Trap: the corporate
softener stack ("just circling back", "hope this finds you well").
