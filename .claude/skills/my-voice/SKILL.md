Voice is not described, it is sourced. Load-bearing sentences come from
specimens (him talking, unedited); the model writes connective tissue
only; the voice pass cuts toward his cadence. This skill holds the
specimen bank, the lens, and one dial file per medium. It is called by
instrument-ship at Stage 2 for readings and room cards.

## Always load
1. `references/voice.md` — specimens, observable markers, the dominant
   finding (the voice is INTERROGATIVE), the ban list, the voice pass.
2. `references/lens.md` — how he sees any subject (absence-first). A piece
   that names no absence is not through his lens.

## Then load exactly one medium file from `references/media/`
Pick by where the words will live. Each file sets the dials: registers
pulled, length, question density, story presence, playful level, ending,
and the medium's specific traps. If no file matches, use the closest and
say so in one line.

## The invariants (every medium)
- Interrogative spine: it asks; when it states, the statement was earned
  by a question just before it.
- One concrete number where one is true. Never invent one for rhythm.
- Stories as replayed dialogue — HIS side quoted, the other party's side
  described, never quoted, never their employer's internals (numbers,
  systems, decisions). The story is his to tell; theirs isn't.
- Reactions stay in where a counterpart exists ("Wow, okay." "I hate it.").
- The ban list in voice.md is absolute.
- Ends on a question or the client question — never a takeaway triad or
  a CTA — unless the medium file says otherwise.

## The voice pass (before anything ships)
1. Skeleton first: place verbatim specimen lines where the weight is.
2. Draft connective tissue only.
3. Read aloud; cut 20–30%. His voice is subtractive.
4. Run the ban list.
5. Counterparty check: nothing quoted or exposed from the other side.
6. Blind test when stakes are high: 3 his + 3 drafted, shuffled.

## Growing the bank
`references/transcript-mining.md` — how raw transcripts become
specimens, lines, lens evidence, and more. Quotation only. Redaction
pre-pass at corpus scale. Never source voice from a cleaned transcript.

## Refusals
- "Make it sound professional" → the ban list is what professional
  sounds like; decline to add it, offer the read-aloud test instead.
- Third-person bio → first person, defined by the gap; say why.
- A draft with no specimen underneath → mark it [drafted] and ask for
  60 seconds of him talking about it instead.
