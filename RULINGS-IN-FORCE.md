## The new rule that stops this repeating

The old log had good discipline — **46 of 72 rulings carried a revisit trigger.** They
were not careless.

The triggers pointed at things like *"the loop #139 sweep"*, *"the five-session pilot"*,
*"the 14-day criteria."* All of that machinery stopped running. So the triggers never
fired, and rulings written to expire became permanent by accident.

**The fix: a ruling whose revisit trigger points at something that no longer exists
expires by default and comes back for a yes or no.**

Same principle as Telegraph+: *a belief must continuously earn the right to survive.*
