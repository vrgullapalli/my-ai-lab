## Conflicts with this direction, found 2026-09-12

Each one is recorded, not smoothed over. None is resolved here unless a row above says so.

1. **Standing watch first, or retrieval first.** The possibility brief (2026-09-11, section 6) ranked a standing watch as the highest-value move. Seed A-LIVE-293 and this direction put retrieval first. Resolved by AD-06, his word. The brief stays as written; it is a dated record.
2. **"Capability" means four things in the lab.** Career model (92, about him), Six Capability Pillars (brand lens), Telegraph's function list, and now lab-system capabilities. Handled by AD-04 (different ids) and by one paragraph at the top of the map. Not a ruling; a naming fence.
3. **Continuity breaks the registry standard.** "Waiting on Venkat" has three homes. `waiting.py` reads them as one view, which hides the fault rather than fixing it. Registered as degraded. Fix not designed.
4. **The routines record disagrees with the account.** `work-os/scheduled-tasks/README.md` says "28 cloud routines"; the account listed 2 on 2026-09-11. Registered as degraded. His ruling on the missing routines is still open (since 2026-09-11 15:20). The hand test adds a reason not to recreate them as they were.
5. **The front door loads fixed context.** `CLAUDE.md` plus `how-i-work.md` load every session, about 320 lines. AD-01 challenges this. Nothing changes until context assembly (step 5). His own item "Trim the front door" (2026-09-11 23:37) points the same way.
6. **Seven jobs, eight families, three rings.** The possibility brief describes the lab as seven jobs and three rings. The inspection here used eight families. They do not fight: families were the lens for inspection, the registry holds capabilities, and the three rings (hard core, reasoning, human) show up as the deterministic, AI, and human lines in every definition.
7. **`DONE.md` already asks for "Retrieval performance is measured."** Written 2026-08-11. Whether `DONE.md` is still the bar is open (`RULINGS-IN-FORCE.md` Part 3, question 2). The retrieval definition meets that line either way.
8. **Alfred's authority.** `AUTHORITY.md` says any file change outside his log is "prepares, Venkat approves." Today's files were written at his explicit ask, so they are prepared work, not approved work. The home (AD-09) and the two proposed rows (AD-04, AD-05) wait on him.
9. **The open routine did not run first.** This was the first session of 2026-09-12 and the hook asked for `alfred-open`. The ask above was treated as the urgent message. The open routine is still owed.
10. **`context/how-i-work.md` changed under this session.** At 00:54 on 2026-09-12, one minute after this session opened, the file went from the live version ("LIVE since 2026-09-11 23:37") back to a longer draft whose front matter says "DRAFT. Not loaded yet." `git diff` shows 159 lines added, 57 removed. This session did not touch it; other Claude Code sessions were running (started 00:24 and 00:32). The front door still imports it as live. Reported, not fixed. His call.
11. **The first run of the competing-store check found one.** `evidence-record` and `continuity` both claimed `evidence/receipts` as canonical store. Fixed at 01:25: evidence-record owns receipts; continuity reads the follow-up lines in them. The check caught what two readings of the map this morning did not.
