# The AI Lab Portfolio: target architecture

## 0. What this file settles

The doctrine says how the lab behaves: five Products, one connective intelligence layer, State and Authority underneath, Noticing and Learning across. It does not say what the Portfolio is made of. So today a capability lives in the career model, a prospect lives in a target folder, an idea lives in the seedbank, a public asset lives in a registry, and nothing connects them. No outcome has ever been recorded. No signal has ever reached action.

This file answers five questions:

1. What is the Portfolio for, and what makes it different from the career model, State, and evidence.
2. What durable things exist in it, how each one changes, and where each one is canonical today.
3. How those things relate, without a schema.
4. Which intelligence domains understand them, and how Portfolio Intelligence reads across all of them.
5. Which few operating loops make the Portfolio change and compound, and how today's lab maps onto the target.

**Rules this file keeps.** The doctrine's Product definitions and boundaries are not reopened. The career model stays the canonical capability spine and nothing writes into it from inference (AD-31, ruling 038). There is no universal Portfolio database. Nothing has to earn its right to exist; tests judge implementations (AD-25). What must exist is separated from how big its first form needs to be.

**His corrections of 2026-09-14, applied throughout.** The first draft of this file went to him the same day. He accepted the object model as the working Portfolio model and corrected six things:

1. **Goal / Direction is a required Portfolio responsibility, but the four current drivers are directional inputs, not canonical Goal objects.** No Goal system is designed yet. The architecture is not wired around the current four.
2. **Durable domain truth is written to the canonical object or evidence source, under Authority.** State records only current operating condition, status, unresolved condition, and other cross-cutting overlays. It is never the home of a fact about the world.
3. **Shared Intelligence proposes interpretations and changes.** It does not use State as a universal destination for durable intelligence. A durable conclusion lands in the object it is about, or in evidence, when Authority allows; State carries only its status.
4. **"The move" is a cross-cutting Portfolio decision rule, applied before consequential moves, not a loop of its own.** Five operating loops remain: Market, Commercial, Public, Capability, Portfolio Reading.
5. **Commercial Opportunity is a durable Portfolio object. Public-value opportunity is a gate inside the Public loop,** not the same object.
6. **Canonical-home status has four categories:** canonical home exists · partial or split home · no current home · intentionally deferred or private. No binary count.

**His two final corrections, 11:46 the same day, applied throughout.**

7. **Experiment and Option are separate durable categories.** An Experiment is a deliberate test run to change what we know. An Option is a path deliberately kept open because it may become valuable later. They have different lifecycles and different jobs (learning; optionality) and are never merged again. The working model has sixteen categories, not fifteen.
8. **Expression is not a Portfolio object.** An expression (a post, a page, a deck, an article version) is a manifested form of an Asset, tracked on the Asset that it expresses and, when one exists, on the Campaign that used it. No Expression system, object, or registry.

**The AI-native definition used.** The doctrine's exact four criteria, section 0 of the revised file:

1. The job or way of working is redesigned.
2. AI is part of the core design.
3. Remove AI and the job or way of working no longer exists as designed; a human or a fundamentally different non-AI process would have to take over.
4. The main value can be a new way of working.

The Portfolio as a whole (section 1) and every operating loop (section 5) are tested against all four.

## 1. The Portfolio: definition and purpose

**Definition (proposed).** The Portfolio is everything Venkat has built, earned, learned, or kept open that can move a goal, held as one connected picture and read against the direction he still owns. The working spec calls it "usable accumulated capacity." That is the right phrase.

**The job it must enable.** Judge each move by what it leaves behind. His own move, the lab's move, a product's move, a piece of writing, a reply to a prospect: each one is read for what it creates, reuses, or puts at risk over time. The doctrine's Portfolio promise: "Today's move is judged by tomorrow's starting position." In this file that judgment is one cross-cutting decision rule (section 5, before the loops), applied before any consequential move in any loop.

**Direction is required; the current drivers are inputs.** The Portfolio must always have something to read moves against. Today that is the four current drivers in `context/intent/STANDING.md`, which he ruled are "current drivers of the lab," replaceable without touching the machinery. They are the directional input the Portfolio reads now. They are not Goal objects, and no Goal system is designed here. When a loop needs more than a driver gives (a horizon, a priority, a relationship to another goal), that is the trigger to design one, and it is his word.

**What makes it distinct.** Four things already hold parts of this, and each one is not the Portfolio:

| Thing | What it holds | Why it is not the Portfolio |
|---|---|---|
| Career model | What he has done and can do, as approved, evidence-tagged record (92 capabilities, 5 domains, 13 products, frozen 2026-08-31) | It looks backward on purpose. Its register entry says it is "not-alone" for "where he is heading." It is the capability spine the Portfolio reads, never the whole. |
| State | What is true now: active, decided, open, waiting, owned, superseded | It records current condition and judges nothing. The Portfolio reads it over time. |
| Evidence and Retrieval | What happened, with pointers; what the lab already holds on a question | Evidence is the raw material. The Portfolio is the reading of what it adds up to. |
| Seedbank | Ideas he wrote, said, or endorsed (825 files) | Ideas are one input. A seed becomes portfolio when it becomes an asset, a product, or a belief in use. |

**What the Portfolio adds that none of them hold.** Connection (this work served that goal and strengthened that asset). Current relevance (this capability is the active offer; that one is dormant). Compounding (what remains after the work is done). Concentration (too much rests on one client, one product, one idea). Optionality (what is still open because of a past choice). Drift (recent choices no longer match a stated goal).

**The Portfolio tested against the four criteria.**

| Criterion | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 1. Redesigned | Today "is this compounding" is asked by hand at the end of a project, if at all; the career model is a record; outcomes are an empty folder. In the target, the question is asked at the move, and the answer connects the move to goals, capacity, and cost. |
| 2. AI in the core design | Connecting a piece of work to a goal and to the assets it strengthens or duplicates. Seeing that six activities are one loop. Seeing that an opportunity advances one goal and works against another. A script cannot do these; it can only count. |
| 3. Remove AI | What remains is a catalog of assets, a list of four drivers, and a monthly review he would have to run himself. That is the shape the lab has today, and it has produced zero recorded outcomes. |
| 4. New way of working | Reuse noticed when a thing is made, not rediscovered months later. Tension between goals shown before a commitment. The economic loop (work, retained capital, proof, access, new paid use) watched as one loop. |

Passes. The reading is the product. The records underneath stay records.

**Three things that are not Portfolio objects, on purpose.**

- **Signals** (the market-signal briefs, 105 backfilled files) are evidence about the market. They feed Market Intelligence. A signal becomes a Portfolio object only when it becomes an opportunity or an outcome. The 2026-09-11 scan's rule stands: signals do not automatically become seeds, beliefs, or actions.
- **Commitments, open loops, and today's list** are State. They describe the current condition of work, not durable capacity.
- **Expressions** (a LinkedIn post, a page, a PDF, an article version, a deck) are manifested forms of an Asset, not objects of their own (his correction 8). An expression is recorded on the Asset it expresses and, when a Campaign used it, on that Campaign. The asset doctrine already in force says the same: a post is an expression of an asset, not a new asset. Outcomes that come back from an expression (readers, replies, inbound) are written to the Asset and the Campaign, never to the expression.

**The one-home rule.** Each object has one canonical home. Everything else that lists the same thing is a view and says so (registry standard, `CAPABILITY-MAP.md` section 3). Where a home is missing, this file says none. It does not pick the folder; that is an implementation choice under AD-33.

**Where durable truth is written (his correction 2).** A fact about the world (this account said this, this engagement delivered that, this capability is evidenced here) is written to the canonical object it belongs to, or to evidence-record, under Authority. State records only the current operating condition of that object: its status, what is open or unresolved about it, who owns it, what supersedes what, and other cross-cutting overlays no registry holds (AD-37). A durable truth never lives only in State. If the object has no home yet, the truth waits in evidence (a receipt, a transcript anchor) with a State overlay saying "unplaced," and the missing home is the finding.

## 3. How the things relate

No schema. These are the relations the loops in section 5 need, in plain words, with the rule for where each relation is recorded.

**The recording rule (his ruling 038, extended).** A relation is written where the work happens, on the side that changed, never inside the canonical model it points at. A capability match is recorded in the dossier, the brief, or the engagement record, with the CAP id and the extract, never in `capabilities.json`. The same holds for every object: the newer, more specific record points at the older, more canonical one.

## 4. The Shared Intelligence domains that understand the Portfolio

The doctrine names ten intelligences inside Shared Intelligence (section 7 of the revised file). This section says what each one reads in the Portfolio, what it produces, and what its nearest thing in the lab is today. An intelligence is not a store. It is a standing, reasoned, current understanding of one kind of thing, with its own provenance and marks. It reads its objects through Retrieval and Context. It proposes interpretations and changes; it commits nothing.

**Where an intelligence's conclusions go (his correction 3).** A durable conclusion (this posting matches this capability; this asset duplicates that one; this account's need changed) is a proposal until Authority accepts it, and then it is written to the object it is about (the dossier, the asset record, the engagement) or to evidence-record, under the recording rule in section 3. State receives only the overlay: that the proposal is open, accepted, or superseded, and what its status is. State is never the default destination for durable intelligence. If an intelligence has nowhere to put a durable conclusion because the object has no home, that is a finding, not a reason to write it into State.

**What must exist for each one (architectural necessity).** A question the system can answer for that kind of thing, an object or objects it reads, and a canonical object or evidence source where its accepted conclusions land. **What its first form can be (implementation).** One reasoning pass on demand, over the existing sources, through the Retrieval and Context capabilities that already exist. No intelligence needs a system of its own.

## 5. The operating loops

One decision rule and five loops (his correction 4). The rule is applied before any consequential move inside any loop. Each loop is the smallest loop through which one part of the Portfolio changes and compounds. Every loop ends in an outcome and a write-back, which is why the empty outcomes folder is the first thing to fix. Each loop names the Product with primary responsibility at each step, per the doctrine, and is tested against the four criteria.

### The move: a cross-cutting decision rule, not a loop

**The rule.** Before a consequential move is taken, in any loop, read it against the direction and the accumulated capacity, and judge it by what it leaves behind. A consequential move is one that is external, hard to reverse, or spends more than a session. The read uses the compounding lens: immediate progress, reuse, capability growth, evidence, access, optionality, future cost, reversibility.

**Who does what.** Process brings the candidate move from conditions. Portfolio Intelligence supplies the read. Portfolio (the Product) judges: this move, a narrower one, or a different one. Trust says how strong the reading is. Experience shows him only the judgment and the one line of why. The move and its result go to the record they belong to (the opportunity, the engagement, the asset, the build); State records only that the move is active, and later its outcome status.

**Stays a script.** Reuse counts. Direction changes only by his word. The record of the move.

**His.** The move itself when it is external or irreversible; any change to direction.

**Why a rule and not a loop.** It has no objects of its own and no trigger of its own; it fires inside every loop at the moment a move becomes consequential. Making it a loop would mean a second place that picks moves, beside Process.

**Today.** The morning brief with one prepared next action is the first proving loop (AD-30). It picks by urgency and follow-up age. It does not read the move against direction or ask what it leaves behind. The State package is built; the brief is not yet wired to it (open commitment). The rule first runs there.

**The rule against the four criteria** (it is not a loop, but it is judgment, so it is tested). (1) Redesigned: from "what is oldest and open" to "what advances the direction and leaves something behind." (2) AI in the core: judging which direction a move serves and what it strengthens or duplicates is interpretation. (3) Remove AI: the brief goes back to a sorted list of follow-ups, and the compounding question goes back to a monthly review he runs himself. (4) New way of working: the compounding question asked at every consequential move, not at the end of a project. Passes.

### Loop 1. The market: a change out there becomes a commercial opportunity, or silence

**Trigger.** A signal lands (a brief, a posting, a news item, a competitor move, a regulation), or a target's condition changes.

**Steps.** Noticing sees the change and applies the seven-question threshold; default silence. Market or Account Intelligence says what changed and what it connects to. Capability Intelligence matches the need to a capability, with CAP id and extract, and states what the model cannot answer. Opportunity Intelligence reads fit against direction, assets, and cost. The decision rule runs if pursuing it would be consequential. Trust marks the read (observed, inferred). Experience shows: what changed, what it fits, needs you or not. Process opens a commercial opportunity or reroutes work already open. The opportunity is written to its home (once it has one) under Authority, with the match recorded on the dossier; State records only that it is open and what depends on it.

**Objects touched.** Account, Commercial opportunity (created), Capability (read), Direction (read), Relationship (read for a warm path).

**Stays a script.** Which routines ran; which briefs were pulled; freshness; scope refusal (a personal or client-confidential source never feeds a public read).

**His.** Whether to pursue. Registering a new source of signals (AD-21).

**Today.** Signals are gathered by cloud routines that are documented as 28 and alive as 2, that cannot open web pages, and whose output nothing in the lab reads. "No signal has ever reached action." The hand test of 2026-09-12 ran this loop once by hand with the lab in view and wrote three moves. The ruling on the routines has been open since 2026-09-11.

**Four criteria.** (1) Redesigned: from gather-then-reason-blind-then-stop at a page nobody reads, to gather, then reason in the lab with capabilities, goals, and accounts in view. (2) AI in the core: matching a market need to a capability by meaning, and judging fit against the whole Portfolio. (3) Remove AI: a keyword match against postings, where the broadest capabilities always win (the one demand run put a generic translation capability on top) and his differentiators never match because buyers do not use his words. A person would read every brief by hand. (4) New way of working: opportunities noticed against what he can prove, not against what he is called. Passes.

### Loop 2. The commercial loop: opportunity, engagement, outcome, and what it leaves behind

**Steps.** Process moves the work through the stages the engagement needs, not a fixed sequence. Account and Relationship Intelligence keep what has been said and what is owed. Capability Intelligence supplies proof at the grade the claim needs (documented, demonstrated, or higher). Trust sets how strong a claim may be at each consequence (a public number never stands without its receipt). Authority holds the line: nothing external without his word. At the end, the engagement produces outcome, revenue, proof, relationship, and reusable material, each written to the engagement record under Authority; State carries only the engagement's status. Portfolio reads what it left behind and whether concentration rose. Learning keeps what repeated.

**Objects touched.** Commercial opportunity (closed as won, lost, declined), Engagement (created), Outcome, Revenue, Proof (graded), Relationship, Asset (if something reusable came out).

**Stays a script.** Nothing external without approval. Confidentiality: client names stay in scope. Proof-grade rules (a claim cannot reach "demonstrated" without an artifact). Counts of reuse and concentration.

**His.** Every approach, proposal, and price. Opening the contracts store and the message export (proof gaps G-02, G-03).

**Four criteria.** (1) Redesigned: from a pipeline nobody built ("the pipeline it needed was never built," 2026-09-11 scan) to an engagement that carries its own proof, relationship, and reuse from the first conversation. - **"Direction without a Goal object is a hole in the model."** It is a required responsibility served by an input, and the file says so in its home status. The first loop that needs more names the trigger for a Goal object; designing one now would wire the architecture around four drivers he ruled are current, not permanent.
- **"State is the easy place to put a conclusion, so it will end up there anyway."** That is the failure his correction 2 names.
